# boat_rowing friction (2026-10-20, with boat_sail and landing_stage)

1. **The water draws straight through a hull.** The first build put the inner floor 0.12 m under the waterline, and the first EEVEE still on a water plane showed water inside the boat. The engine's water surface does the same, because no runtime masks it. Fix: the floor sits 0.13 m over the keel and the draft is 0.10 m, so every inboard surface is at least 0.03 m over the origin plane (`_boat.INTERIOR_MIN_Z`, asserted per LOD). The runtime notes ask the boat runtime to ride the surface. About 5 min lost. Proposed skill text (project.md, after "Rotors"): **Floating pieces.** A boat's origin plane is the water at rest (the runtime stands it at `WATER_Y`). The engine draws its water surface through any hull, so keep every inboard surface above the origin plane (`_boat.INTERIOR_MIN_Z`, 0.03 m) and assert it per LOD. A static placement does not ride the swell, so in `water_kit` the look-dev waves still show inside a hull. That case is the runtime's to fix (ride the surface, or clip the water inside the hull).
2. **Animatable parts other than a rotor were invisible.** `kit_gate` and both runtimes saw only `<id>_lod<n>` and `<id>_rotor_lod<n>`. Oars and sails as their own nodes would have skipped the budget, tangent, slot, density and hooks checks, and would not be drawn in any shot. Fix (integration.patch): `hex_assets::part_of` and `part_mesh_name`. The gate now counts and checks `<id>_<part>_lod<n>` in every LOD (`part_tris_count_toward_the_lod_budget`), the hooks sampler includes parts, and `kit_scene` draws them at rest. About 15 min. Proposed skill text (project.md, after "Rotors"): **Animatable parts (oars, sails).** A part that the owning runtime moves, rather than one that spins nonstop, is a node and mesh `<id>_<part>_lod<n>` in every LOD with `extras.kind` naming it (`hex_assets::part_of`). Put its node on the pivot and author the mesh in the node frame. Fold its tris into `lodTris`, as with a rotor. Never name it `_rotor_`, because both runtimes spin that name. The kit scenario draws parts at rest. `play.rs` does not draw them, so the runtime that places the piece spawns them. Worked example: `_boat.py`.
3. **`water_kit` water is not at `WATER_Y`.** The look-dev patch draws its water at y = 0, with its own land at +1.4, while play water is at -1.4. Boats placed at -1.4 sat 1.4 m under the look-dev surface. Fix: the ocean-cell boats in the `BOATS` layout stand at y 0, and the river-cell pieces stay at -1.4 against the dry bed. About 5 min. Proposed skill text (pitfalls.md, Look-dev and engine shots): **`water_kit` water sits at y = 0, not at `WATER_Y` (-1.4).** To shoot a floating piece on water in engine, place it at y 0 on the patch's ocean cells (1, 1), (2, 1) and (2, 0). The plain `kit` scenario draws no water.
4. **Cell centres.** I took a cell's z as 16 m per row and missed the river cell in the first kit shots. `hex_core::Axial::to_world` gives x = R√3 (q + r/2) and z = 1.5 R r, which is 24 m per row. About 3 min. No skill change: the function is the reference.
5. **Boom against the shrouds.** The sweep assertion failed at a 74.8° shroud bearing against the 70° main swing, with the chainplates 0.2 m aft of the mast. Moving them abeam gives 85.8°. About 2 min. No skill change: the generator's assertion caught it, which is its job.
6. **Native shots black from inside a script.** `bash docs/checkpoints/boats/run.sh` wrote every native shot black on both of its tries (host load about 350). The same `caffeinate ... target/release/hex-world --script ...` lines, run directly as tool calls one after another, rendered (means 0.14-0.40). One direct kit run also came back black and rendered on retry. Workaround: run the native lines directly and then `bash docs/checkpoints/boats/post.sh` (JPEGs, stills, sheets). About 4 min. Proposed skill text (pitfalls.md, Look-dev and engine shots, after "Native shots can come back black"): Run the native shot lines directly, one per tool call, and put only the post-processing in a script. On 2026-10-20 a shot script run through `bash` from an agent wrote all-black frames twice, while the same lines rendered when run directly (boats).
