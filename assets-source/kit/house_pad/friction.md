# house_pad friction log

1. **The centre/edge joint gate assumed a straight road.** `kit_gate::check_center_edge_joint` required every `center` piece to carry the road section at both x = +6 and x = -6, so a pad whose driveway meets only edge 0 could not pass. Fix: a centre piece that declares `socket-lane-*` nodes is checked at each declared edge's x = +6 joint instead (`lane_socket_edges`, `lod_sections_at_edge`, `lane_joint_errors`); straight centres without lane sockets keep the ±6 pair unchanged. The bends need the same rule. Minutes lost: 15. Skill: `gates.md` "Seam: lanes" row updated.
2. **`scene.reset_scene` leaves no world**, so setting the AO distance (`scene.world.light_settings.distance`) raised `AttributeError`. Fix: create a world first (`scn.world = scn.world or bpy.data.worlds.new(piece)`). Minutes lost: 3. Skill: `pitfalls.md`, "Set the world AO distance before an AO bake".
3. **Buried faces share planar-UV texels.** With one top-down masks UV, the gravel under the driveway and under the doorsteps baked AO 0 into the same texels as the surfaces on top (yard AO p05 = 0). Fix: stop the yard where the driveway starts, and join the doorsteps (like the road paint) after the bake. Yard AO p05 is now 1.0. Minutes lost: 5. Skill: `pitfalls.md`, the decal entry now covers any surface stacked over another under a planar UV.
4. **A sliced 4D Voronoi F1 reads as polka dots, and its cells come out about 5x larger than 1 / scale.** The gravel set's first two bakes were sparse round discs, then crazy paving at 8 cm. Fix: shape packed stones from `DISTANCE_TO_EDGE`, colour them from an F1 node with the same cells, and measure the stone size on the 2×2 tile (Voronoi 40 gives about 2 cm). Minutes lost: 15. Skill: `pitfalls.md`, "Tileable sets".
5. **New anchor kinds are invisible to the export report.** `lib/export.write_export_report` listed only `socket-lane-*` and `anchor-prop-*` empties, so `anchor-park-*` would have been dropped from export-report.json, provenance `sockets` and the Blender reimport check. Fix: add the prefix there (and in kit_gate's extras check). Minutes lost: 2. Skill: `lib-contracts.md` `add_socket` entry lists the parking prefix and its extras.
6. **No convention for "re-exported, awaiting re-approval".** Fix: append a `"status": "pending"` entry to `approvals.json` and keep the earlier approval as history. Minutes lost: 3. Skill: `provenance.md` "Fixed values".
7. **Empties do not show in the six-view renders**, so the parking spots needed their own stills. Fix: `scripts/blender/park_render.py` draws a 5.4 x 2.0 m outline and a nose arrow at each `anchor-park-*` (marks named `*_lod0`, because `render_six_views` renders LOD0 meshes only). Minutes lost: 5. Skill: no change; the script's docstring carries the command.
