# meadow_base friction log

Authoring agent: claude-code / claude-opus-5-5. Covers meadow_base and the grass and soil tileable sets.
Each entry gives what happened, the fix, and the minutes lost.

1. **The plan's mat skeleton writes `generator=__file__` into a committed bake report.** Under `-P`, `__file__` can be an absolute host path, which provenance must never carry. Fix: the generators write `pathlib.Path(__file__).resolve().relative_to(export.REPO)`, and the material-mode assembler re-hashes the generator from the file instead of trusting the report. Skill gap: `lib-contracts.md` documents the relative path for `write_export_report` but not for `write_bake_report`, which tileable sets use. 3 min.
2. **3D textures silently break the wrap.** `torus_coords` only wraps when every texture is 4D (Noise and Voronoi with `*_dimensions = "4D"` and W linked). The 3D default ignores W and mirrors the tile about its middle, and the 2×2 seam test does not show that as a seam. Fix: the generators build every texture through 4D helpers. Skill gap: `lib-contracts.md` should say "4D Noise/Voronoi/White Noise only; Wave, Magic, Brick, Gradient and Checker cannot wrap". 2 min.
3. **Soil strata and the wall mapping.** `kit_common::tile_frame` maps wall V to −y / tileM, so the cap rim (y = 0) sits exactly on the tile's V = 1/0 wrap, and tileM 2.0 repeats the strata twice down the 4 m skirt. Fix: the band coordinate is `fract(V − 0.05 + jitter)`, with V recovered as atan2(w, z) / 2π so it stays a function of the torus coordinates. The rim lands in topsoil, and the topsoil-to-gravel contact sits about 1.9 m below the rim instead of on the tile edge. The 2× repeat is a D2 consequence and is raised at the look-dev checkpoint. Skill gap: `project.md` should state the wall V convention and the rim/wrap alignment for anyone authoring a set that is banded vertically. 6 min.
4. **The worktree guard rejects heredocs and compound commands.** Python heredocs that patch files, and `cmd; python3 …; cargo …` chains, were refused as "too complex to verify". Fix: write the patch scripts as files, then run them with single commands. Not a skill issue, but worth one line under the skill's invocation rules for agents running in worktrees. 4 min.
5. **The first grass bake read as polka dots.** Voronoi tuft cells with a tight distance falloff gave round dots at 512 px/m instead of a clumpy turf. Fix: a soft tuft falloff at low weight, with high-distortion, high-frequency noise blades on top. Lesson for the skill: judge a tileable set from the 2×2 tile at 1:1 before compressing, and keep Voronoi for sparse features (clover, flowers, pebbles). 8 min.

## Terrain-hump pass (2026-10-20, meadow_base, forest_base, forest_forest_edge_e0..e5)
6. **The border hump was shading, not the masks.** The relief's smoothstep falloff lifts the cap from about -0.11 m at 2 m to 0 at the ring, a mean slope toward the border of 4.6 degrees at 0.5-1 m (9.3 at most), on both sides of every border, so side light drew a ridge along the hex grid. masks.A (the only masks channel live at noon) is flat at 0.999-1.0 to the ring and G only acts under the wet hook. Fix: custom normals from the relief's slope without the falloff term (`shade_slope`, `geom.set_cap_normals`), +Z on the ring; geometry, LOD tris and every runtime ground consumer are unchanged. The geometric 0.11 m rise remains: the walker climbs it, and at the 3-degree dusk sun its lee side casts faint shadow streaks. Measuring the cause and testing the normals in engine from a scratch `assets/` clone took 15 min.
7. **`--kit_hooks dusk` works in the play scenario** (the time-of-day block lowers the sun to about 3 degrees) although `docs/agent-operability.md` listed the key as kit-only without `dusk`; it is the grazing-light shot for terrain relief. Doc row corrected. 3 min.
8. **The worktree hook refused a `for … cp …; done; python3 …` line** as a runtime-assembled program; the same loop alone passed. Already covered by SKILL.md "Worktree sessions" (each command alone). 1 min.
9. **forest_forest_edge_e* bake their masks on the base cap's UV, not the planar overlay UV their generator writes.** The overlay keeps forest_base's planar `UVMap` from `bm.from_mesh`, so `uv_layers.new(name="UVMap")` makes a second layer (`UVMap.001`, exported as an unused TEXCOORD_1). The bake, the texel density (16 px/m) and the runtime all use TEXCOORD_0, the base UV, so the shipped pieces shade correctly (A about 1 over the overlay's island), but `bake_masks`' AO stats sample the planar footprint and report mean 0.096 (unbaked background). Not fixed here (the pieces' UV layout is approved and the look is right); a later pass can drop the dead layer and measure the stats on the base UV. Found while checking the masks for a border band. 6 min.
10. **Two of three identical play shot runs were spoiled by the session, not the asset.** The first after run came out about 4% darker at noon everywhere, backdrop included, and the next identical run matched the before run; in that second run the two low noon poses showed a red placement ghost, because a real pointer over the native window hovers a slot whatever the script's `hover` did. Diff every after shot against its before shot (per-region means, `crop.py`) before trusting a change, keep the pointer off the window, and re-run a run that differs everywhere. Proposed SKILL.md line (Owner checkpoints): "Native play shots take the real pointer's hover: keep it off the window and check each shot for a ghost tile." 8 min.
11. **The dusk residual is shadow, verified.** With the normals fixed, faint streaks remained in the dusk orbit; the same pose with `--shadows off` has none (`docs/checkpoints/terrain-hump/crop-dusk-shadows.jpg`), so they are cast shadows of the remaining 0.11 m geometric rise at a 3-degree sun. 4 min.
