# road_bend_60_edge_e0 / road_bend_60_edge_e1 friction

Both stubs come from one helper (`scripts/blender/kit/_road_bend_trim.py`); this log covers both.

1. **A bisect near an existing row leaves a sliver** (0 min lost, caught before the first build). The cut meets the sink foot (t = -4.6) at x = SINK_T * sqrt(3) = 7.967. road_straight_edge's LOD1 has 4 segments, so a row sits at x = 7.964, 3 mm away, and `bmesh.ops.bisect_plane` would leave a 3 mm triangle. LOD1 takes 3 segments instead (90 tris, 35%), and `trim` asserts every triangle is over 1e-4 m2 after the cut. Proposed skill text (`pitfalls.md`, Turning road pieces): "**Cutting a swept piece with `bisect_plane`: keep rows away from the cut.** Where the cut crosses each profile line, check the nearest row in every LOD (the 60-degree trim meets t = -4 at x = 6.928 and t = -4.6 at x = 7.967; road_straight_edge's LOD1 row at 7.964 would leave a 3 mm sliver), pick segment counts that keep them centimetres apart, and assert a minimum triangle area after the cut (`_road_bend_trim.MIN_TRI_AREA`)."
2. **`_road.build`'s triangulation is not mirror-symmetric** (about 10 min). Every profile cell is split along the same diagonal direction, so the -y half is not the mirror of the +y half (no triangle of any LOD has a mirror partner; checked headless). Trimming the full stub with the mirrored plane would cut e1's cells along other diagonals than e0's, and the two cut lines would meet with T-junctions. e1 is therefore e0 mirrored (y -> -y, faces reversed), so both carry the same cut vertices. The cost: e1's 64-sample AO bake runs over mirrored triangles, so its masks A differs from road_straight_edge's at noise level (180 of 18,270 texels away from the cut off by more than 2 levels, at most 20, mean 0.065; R, G and B identical), where e0's matches within 4 levels on 2 texels (`docs/checkpoints/road_bend_60-trim/masks_diff.py`). Proposed skill text (`pitfalls.md`, Turning road pieces): "**A mirrored pair of cut pieces: mirror the mesh, not the cut.** `_road.build` splits every cell along the same diagonal, so its -y half is not the +y half mirrored; cut one piece and build its partner by mirroring the cut mesh (y -> -y, `bmesh.ops.reverse_faces`), so both meet vertex for vertex on the cut (`_road_bend_trim.trim`)."
3. **Tile-specific edge pieces need a scoped joint check** (about 15 min). `kit_gate` compared every centre with every edge piece of the lane's kind, so adding the trimmed stubs failed every other road centre (the straight's x = 6 section is the full profile). The joint check now takes the edge piece the tile places on the lane (`TileDef::edge_pieces`) and keeps the every-piece fallback, without the tiles' `Explicit` pieces, for bases, untiled centres and lanes the tile places nothing on. Proposed skill text (`gates.md`, Seam: lanes): replace "has, at the joint, exactly the section of every edge piece of the same network kind (bed and paint vertices, within 0.1 mm)" with "has, at the joint, exactly the section (bed and paint vertices, within 0.1 mm) of the edge piece the tile places on that lane (`TileDef::edge_pieces`: the socket table's piece, or the tile's own `edge_pieces: Explicit(...)` list, road_bend_60's trimmed stubs), or, on a lane the tile places nothing on, of every edge piece of the same network kind that no tile lists explicitly", and add `kit_gate::joints_meet_the_edge_pieces_the_tile_places` to its "Pinned by" list.
4. **The worktree hook refused a heredoc plus `sed` on one line** (1 min). Known rule (SKILL.md, Worktree sessions); the generators were then written with the Write tool. No change needed.
5. **Shots at host load 650-800** (0 min lost). `make shots-piece` wrote every frame on the first try (no black frames); two identical before runs differed by at most 6 levels per frame (one pixel at 11), the noise floor the before/after comparison is read against.
