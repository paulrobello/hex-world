1. **The frozen road stubs hang over the channel** (about 10 min of design). The road crosses at 60 degrees, so the channel band (|y| <= 6.83) runs under the `road_straight_edge` stubs out to r = 10.7, and a stub's verge corner at r = 6 sits over open water (y = -2.9). No skill change: geometry of this piece.
2. **Material indices set before the slots existed became 0** (10 min). `polygon.material_index = 4` on a mesh with no material slots is clamped to 0 without an error, so the first export put the whole bridge in `road_asphalt` (one primitive), which the gate does not catch. Fix: append the slots first, then set the indices (and keep the indices bmesh wrote, which survive `to_mesh`). Skill: `pitfalls.md`.
3. **The stone slot was briefly colour-only** (counted in river_edge friction 6). Black shots from an idle-asleep display looked like a `plaster` tileable problem; the plaster stone renders at every rotation under `caffeinate`, so it is back. No skill change beyond that entry.
4. **Road joint clearance** (2 min). The gate compares every vertex within 1 mm of r = +-6 with the road edge section, so `joint_clearance` asserts in the generator that no stone or dressing vertex lies within 2 mm of either joint plane. No skill change: covered by `pitfalls.md` (Check the joint in the generator).

## §9.4 hook masks (2026-10-14)

5. **The shared masks map had A = 0 outside the road** (10 min). The AO bake covers only the road bed's islands, so every other texel of the planar 512 px map read A = 0 (mean A 31/255), and the parked causeway texel read (255, 179, 0, 0). Binding the dressing and causeway slots to that map as it was would have multiplied their diffuse light by 0 at noon. Fix: `dress_masks` writes R 1, the river porosity and A 1 over the dressing's own texels (its UV0 footprint grown 3 texels) and a 16 px block at the parked stone UV, and asserts that neither touches a road texel. Compared texel for texel with the approved map, only those 4,007 texels changed (the re-baked road AO is identical). Skill: `pitfalls.md` (A shared masks map with a partial AO bake).
6. **The A = 0 background came back through the mips** (15 min). The box-filtered mip chain blended the unbaked A = 0 texels into the small dressing footprint: the dressing's A fell to a mean of 0.82 at mip 3 and 0.52 at mip 4 (minimum 0.11), so the reeds and bank stones would darken at overview distance. Fix: `dress_masks` also fills a 32 px halo around the dressing with its values, kept 11 px clear of the road's baked texels (islands and gutter). The dressing then holds A of 0.92 or more to mip 4, the road's A is unchanged at mips 0 to 4 (checked against the approved map on every road triangle), and only mip 5 (2 m texels) moves at the road's outer verge. Skill: `pitfalls.md` (same entry).
