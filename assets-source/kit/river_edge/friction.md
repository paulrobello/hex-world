# river_edge friction (river family: river_edge, river_*_base, river_*_center)

1. **The centre/edge joint gate ignored the network kind** (about 15 min). `kit_gate::check_center_edge_joint` compared every `kind: center` piece with every `kind: edge` piece at x = 6, so a river piece would have failed against `road_straight_edge` and the road centres against `river_edge`. Fix (additive, `kit_gate.rs`): an edge piece's network is its lane socket's `extras.kind`; a joint compares only pieces of the same kind (a lane with no declared kind is a road); the joint plane is the edge piece's inner end, measured as its smallest LOD0 x (6 for the road stub, APOTHEM - 1 for the river mouth); sections match within one 0.1 mm unit (`same_section`, the rotation noise). Pinned by `joints_are_scoped_by_network_kind`. Skill: `gates.md` (Seam: lanes).
2. **A channel-cut base cannot pass the base ring rule** (about 10 min of design). `kind: base` demands a ring that maps onto itself under 60 degrees, which a ring with the channel spans left out never does, and a river tile must not use a full meadow ring (it buries the channel). Fix: a base that declares `socket-lane-*` nodes with a `cut` extra is lane-cut: its ring must hold every `border_ring(16)` vertex outside |offset| < cut on those edges, plus only the two bank-top points per cut (`cut_ring_errors`, pinned by `lane_cut_base_ring_leaves_out_only_its_lanes`), and its section at each lane's joint must equal the edge piece's. Skill: `gates.md`.
3. **The 24 m bend arc cannot share a long straight stub** (about 10 min of design). The road bends moved their arcs to meet stubs at r = 6, but the water contract fixes the river bend on the 24 m arc. A stub STUB_L long differs from that arc by STUB_L^2 / 48 at its inner end, so `river_edge` is 1 m long (0.021 m in position, under 0.011 m in height) and the bases own the rest of the channel. Skill: `pitfalls.md` (Turning road pieces, river note).
4. **bmesh refuses a back face on the same vertices** (2 min). `faces.new((v1, v0, v2))` after `faces.new((v0, v1, v2))` raises "face already exists". Double-sided cards need their own vertices. Skill: `pitfalls.md`.
5. **A shared helper edit stales every piece that imports it** (5 min). Provenance hashes every imported `_*.py`, so dressing code that lived in `_river.py` would have staled the already-gated base and stub on each edit. Split into `_river.py` (profile, bases, stub), `_river_dress.py` (stones, reeds) and `_river_bridge.py` (the bridge). Skill: `pitfalls.md`.
6. **Black `play` shots sent me down a false bisect** (about 60 min lost). Whole runs wrote every shot black (1600x1000 PNGs of 25,867 B) and the pattern seemed to follow tile rotation, `--shadows`, a manifest entry, `river_edge`'s mouth wall and a world-space tileable slot on the bridge, so I parked the wall's UVs and made the bridge stone colour-only. A later 18-rotation sweep went fully black, then fully rendered with the same binary and data, and back-to-back runs of one binary on one tree went black and rendered in turn, even under `caffeinate -u -d`: the black frames are intermittent and environmental (`pitfalls.md`, Native shots can come back black, names display sleep as one cause; host load was 40-110 throughout). Every river tile renders at all six rotations with the plaster stone restored, in at least one run each. The parked wall UVs stay (they match the meadow skirt and the slot samples no UV map). Skill: `pitfalls.md` (the black-shot bullet now says an idle display also fakes a bisect).
7. **Rotation sweep for every water tile** (method). Three non-adjacent cells around the starting meadow, each reached by `hover` plus the right number of `rotate cw` presses (the ghost cycles legal rotations in ascending order), cover all six rotations of `river_straight`, `river_bend` and `river_road_bridge` in six runs. No skill change: the scripts are scratch.
8. **The worktree hook refused a long heredoc append** (1 min). Used the Edit tool. Already covered by SKILL.md (Worktree sessions); no change.

## Terrain-hump pass (2026-10-20, river_straight_base, river_bend_base, river_spring_base)
9. **The bend's joint rows are not on the arc, so a strip test on `dist_bend` misses two vertices** (5 min). The straight joint row at x = APOTHEM - 1 sits 0.016 m outside the 24 m arc's offset at its outer bank top (dist_bend 6.846 against BANK_TOP 6.83), so the first `shade_slope` gave that joint vertex the meadow slope and changed its normal by 2.5 degrees on LOD2, where no meadow vertex exists. Found by diffing every LOD primitive's normals against the committed GLB (`docs/checkpoints/terrain-hump-water/glbdiff.mjs`). Fix: the strip test allows 0.05 m past BANK_TOP (meadow lattice points start at least 0.42 m beyond it). Proposed `pitfalls.md` line (Water beds): "A normals-only change to a lane-cut base must leave the channel strip and the joint rows alone: classify vertices with margin (the bend's joint rows sit 0.016 m off the arc) and diff every LOD primitive's normals against the committed GLB, not only the band you meant to change."
10. **A shared-helper fix that changes three pieces stales thirteen, and the ten unchanged ones rebuild byte-identical** (10 min). `_river.py` is a generatorDep of every river base, centre and bridge, so rebuild, compress, determinism and provenance ran on all 13. Builds take 1-4 s each here, so the whole set took about 10 minutes of gates; the ten pieces whose output did not change came out byte-identical GLBs and maps (git shows no change under `assets/kit/p1/` for them), which is the cross-process determinism check. No skill change: `stages.md` already says rebuild what goes stale.
11. **The water-family hump was half the meadow's and is subtle in engine** (method). The river bases' relief is 0.10 m deep (spring 0.06) against the meadow's 0.25, so the shipped LOD0 normals leaned 1.57 degrees toward the border at 0.5-1 m (bend 1.38, spring 0.91) against the meadow's 3.6. In play the change moves about 0.5% of a seam close-up's pixels by 2 or more levels, along the border bands only; a per-pixel diff map (`diffmap.py`) shows it where a side-by-side pair does not. The coast and mouth bases' land is flat (0.00 degrees and z 0.000 in every band), so `_beach.py` and `_mouth.py` were left alone.
