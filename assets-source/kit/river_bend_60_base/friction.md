# river_bend_60_base friction

Shared with the river family: see `assets-source/kit/river_edge/friction.md` and `assets-source/kit/river_bend_base/friction.md`.

1. **The edge-midpoint arc does not meet the frozen stub** (5 min, design). The arc about corner 0 through both edge midpoints (8 m, one control point, as `river_bend` does with its 24 m arc) sits L^2 / 2R = 0.063 m off the straight 1 m `river_edge` stub at the joint, about 0.06 m of bed height at the waterline (slope 1.0 m/m there), twice the 0.03 m wet tolerance. The 24 m bend's 0.021 m only fit because its radius is three times larger. Fix: the arc tangent to both stubs at the joints, R = JOINT_X tan 30 deg = 7.4226 m about (12.856, 7.423) runtime, and the tile network as a polyline (the joints plus the arc every 2.5 degrees, chord sag 0.002 m), which `water::tiles::river_segments` and `hex_sim::geom::segment_shape` read as given. The bed is then exact on the composite line, stubs included. Skill: proposed `pitfalls.md` text (Water beds) in the report.
2. **`_river.build_base` prints its numbers after its asserts** (5 min, 2 builds). A failing ratio assert hid the profile errors. `_river_bend_60.profile_errors` wraps the helper's function (a module global) and prints each LOD's triangles, errors and worst wet point before the strict check. No skill change: the skill already asks for this ("Print per-LOD tris ... before each assert").
3. **LOD2 rows are set by the outer bank's chord sag** (15 min, 6 builds). The outer waterline runs 11.4 m from the arc centre and turns 120 degrees: 16 rows gave wet 0.037 m, 20 rows 0.0299 m (784 tris). Stepping the inner offsets to every second row (they crowd at the 0.59 m knob) is safe only where the strip ends inside them: stepping the wet offsets down to -0.8 let the CDT fan a row across two offset intervals (0.19 m). `_river_bend_60.steps` steps only offsets within MIN_ALONG and the sag budget, and LOD2's dry -5.7 offset takes every fourth row. LOD0 lattice spacing comes from an offline count of `hexgeo.lattice_points` under `channel_cap`'s filter (`target/`-free scratch script), which put LOD0 at 3,970 so LOD2 sits at 19.7% of it. Skill: proposed `pitfalls.md` text in the report.
   **Dense check (owner question).** `_river.profile_errors` samples vertices and face centroids. Re-sampled at 15 barycentric points per triangle (`docs/checkpoints/river_bend_60/dense_profile.py`), wet max is LOD0 0.0251, LOD1 0.0278 and LOD2 0.0330 m (outer bank, d = 3.25 m, between the 3.031 kink and 3.5): LOD2 is 3 mm over the 0.03 m contract between sample points. The approved `river_bend_base` measures 0.0422 at every LOD on the same check (near its straight joint rows) and its LOD2 strip models at 0.033 mid-arc, so the family's eight half-offsets miss 0.03 at dense sampling on every bend. An offline model of the strip (both diagonals, the same 15 points; it reproduces the Blender 0.0330) gives the cheapest dense-compliant LOD2 as 20 rows with offsets 1.4 and 3.25 added (wet 0.027, about 940 tris), which needs LOD0 >= 4,700 at the 20% ceiling: over the 4,000 cap. 24 rows alone gives 0.031 (about 900 tris). Shipped at family parity; the owner picks.
4. **The first family file made the base hash the dressing helper** (5 min, 1 rebuild). `_river_bend_60.py` imported `_river_dress` (for the `centre_frame` override) and `_river_flat`, so both pieces listed every river helper in `generatorDeps`. Split: `_river_bend_60.py` (layout, distance, rows; imports `_river` only), `_river_bend_60_dress.py` (the dressing override and the inner-bank thinning), and the base imports `_river_flat` itself as `river_bend_base.py` does. GLB SHA-256 unchanged by the split. No skill change: `pitfalls.md` already says "Split shared helpers by who imports them".

The fork's River edge 5 faces the kit cell this piece first took at rot 0, so the cell turned to rot 1 to join the straight on (-2, 4) to the fork. A full `cargo test --workspace --no-fail-fast` on the new tip also showed counts stale since the wide rail bend (`tile_counts` 33 against 35 cells, the verge list without `rail_bend_120`, the audio land list without it) and one failure this patch does not touch (`buffer_stops_stand_at_open_rail_ends`: the wide rail bend on (-4, 7) now meets the buffer stop's open end on (-3, 6)).
