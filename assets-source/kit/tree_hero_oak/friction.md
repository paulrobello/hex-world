# tree_hero_oak friction log

Snags hit while authoring the hero oak through the live-Blender interactive route. Each entry: what happened, the fix, minutes lost. Each is folded into the blender-asset skill (last section).

1. **The desktop Blender connector timed out.** `mcp__Blender__*` (the Claude desktop add-on) never answered while the owner's Blender ran the uvx `blender-mcp` add-on on 9876. Fix: `scripts/blender/mcp_live.py`, a stdlib socket client (`info` / `exec <file>`), with every live step kept as a file under `live/`. The stages.md Interactive route now names this transport. Lost: about 20 min (first agent).
2. **The worktree isolation hook refuses compound shell commands.** `cd … && git …`, heredocs feeding a command, `sed` with a shell variable, and `awk` programs were all refused as "too complex to verify". Fix: one plain command per call, Edit/Write for file changes, absolute paths. Lost: about 10 min.
3. **Leaf strip only 11% opaque.** The leaf mask used a 4D Voronoi sliced at a fixed W (the tile variant). The F1 distance then includes the off-slice distance to every feature point, so most cells fail `radius > distance` and the clusters read as sparse round blobs; at alphaCutoff 0.5 the canopy thinned and vanished from mip 7 on. Fix: a 2D Voronoi on per-variant offset UVs, keeping the 4D noise for the lobed leaf edge and the cluster outline, tuned with a headless strip-bake sweep to 0.618 opaque at mip 0 (0.62 to 1.0 down the chain). Lost: about 15 min.
4. **Bark AO mean 0.55, min 0.** Not the leaves: `bake_to` already hides every mesh outside `low` + `highs`, and the AO call passes only the bark high. Two causes, measured with a 512² diagnostic bake: the world AO distance was never set (Blender default 10 m, so limbs 4 m up and the other trunk strands darkened everything: mean 0.547, 25% of texels at 0), and receiver faces buried inside the intertwined trunk strands, root starts and limb starts bake to 0 (projection misses were only 1%, so the cage was fine). Fix: `AO_DIST = 0.35` m set on the world before the AO bake and recorded as `aoDistanceM`, plus `_tree.cull_buried`, which deletes receiver faces whose every vertex lies inside another branch's inscribed low tube. Result: bark AO mean 0.763. Lost: about 20 min.
5. **Culling vs the texel-density median.** Halving the trunk/root receiver step let `cull_buried` drop 37 instead of 12 faces, but `measure_texel_density` takes an unweighted per-face median, so the extra bark faces dragged the LOD0 median from 270 to 115 px/m and failed the 256 assert. Reverted to the coarse step. The 256 px/m median holds only because leaf cards outnumber bark faces; bark itself is about 108 px/m (a 2048² atlas cannot hold unique bark at 256 px/m). Worth an area-weighted density metric. Lost: about 5 min.
6. **`collider_from(kind="hull")` raised on a hull-of-a-hull.** `bmesh.ops.convex_hull` can list a vertex as both unused and interior, and `bmesh.ops.delete` rejects duplicates (`ValueError: found the same … used multiple times`). Fix in `lib/collider.py` (de-duplicate verts) with the regression test `test_collider`, which failed before the fix. Lost: about 5 min.
7. **Removed objects linger in `view_layer.objects` as `None`.** After `bpy.data.objects.remove` on a freshly opened file, `scene.select_only` iterated a `None` base. Fix: `bpy.context.view_layer.update()` after the removals. Lost: about 3 min.
8. **Wind UV layer order.** `uv.atlas_unwrap` and `pack_islands` act on the active UV layer, so the `wind` map must be created after the pack, with `UVMap` re-set active and first. Collapse decimation also overshoots interpolated UVs by about 3e-4, so wind is clamped to [0, 1] after the LOD joins. The glTF exporter writes V as 1 − v (phase is cyclic, so harmless). Lost: about 5 min.
9. **Provenance for the interactive route dropped the generator hashes.** `kit-provenance.mjs` wrote `blendRef` instead of `generator`/`generatorDeps`, so the scripted export stage and `_tree.py` were unhashed, and the key was `relative(ROOT, …)`, which gives `../../../…` for the main-checkout store. Fix: both blocks, a store-relative key via `--blend-store`, and a test. Lost: about 5 min.
10. **Host load 38-150 during the shot window.** Other sessions kept the 1-minute load far above 15. The shots waited in bounded stretches (see the report for readings).

## Authoring decisions (as shipped, owner-approved 5bc77f1)
- **High-poly construction.** Tubes swept along a seeded skeleton (three intertwined trunk strands, seven root flares, six limbs plus a leader, secondaries and tertiaries) with noise displacement along the ring normal, instead of a skin modifier: the same skeleton then yields the LOD0 receiver at low side counts, so the bake projects cleanly.
- **LOD1/LOD2 use the brief's leaf-subset fallback.** Collapse-decimating the cards destroys the canopy, so LOD1 keeps every 2nd fringe card scaled 1.25× and LOD2 every 4th scaled 1.5× (`card_subset` in `tree_hero_oak.py`), and only the bark is decimated to fill the remaining ratio. (The first pass used every 2nd at 1.3× and every 6th at 1.9×.)
- **Leaf atlas.** The fringe-card leaf tiles fill the strip v ≥ `LEAF_V0` (0.75) of the 2048² atlas, drawn in numpy by `_leaves.strip` (entry 11) and written directly, with no leaf bake. Bark islands (UV-mapped tileable set, entry 12) and the canopy shell bake (entries 20-22) pack below the strip. (The first pass self-baked four cluster variants from flat tile quads.)

## Look pass (owner, after the first checkpoint)
11. **Leaves as round blobs.** A shader-node leaf mask cannot draw a lobed oak outline without a pile of math nodes. Fix: `_leaves.py` draws the four cluster tiles in numpy (220 lobed, obovate oak leaves per tile, back to front, with per-leaf tone, a midrib and a folded normal). The live session loads the same arrays as packed images, so the showcase and the engine show the same leaves, and the export stage writes the strip directly (no leaf bake). Coverage 0.565 at mip 0, rising to 1.0 at the last mips. Lost: about 10 min.
12. **World-space tiling cannot map a trunk.** `kit_common::tile_frame` projects onto a tangent plane per normal, so on a vertical cylinder centred on the axis U collapses to a constant. Fix: a `tileUvScale` slot option. The bark is unwrapped per branch in metres, scaled by `uvPerM`, packed unrotated and unscaled, and StandardMaterial's `uv_transform` multiplies UV0 by K at runtime; masks keep raw UV0 for the AO. No new binding (16/7 held). Lost: about 15 min of design.
13. **Gate pooled median hid low bark density.** Replaced in the generator and the gate by a per-material median (the gate reads the PNG width from IHDR and multiplies by `tileUvScale`). A deliberately wrong K = 4 fails with `tree_hero_oak_bark median texel density 177.9 px/m < 256 (per material)`. Lost: about 5 min.
14. **Deeper bark relief broke the AO cage.** With 0.07 m ridged displacement on the high poly and a 6-sided receiver, cage 0.12 m missed (AO mean 0.66, p05 0). Cage 0.25 m / max ray 0.5 m: AO mean 0.72, p05 0.06. Lost: about 5 min.
15. **Zero tangents from up-biased canopy normals.** Biasing foliage normals toward +Z put some custom normals in their card's plane, MikkTSpace returned a zero tangent and gltf-validator failed `kit-compress` (`ACCESSOR_VECTOR3_NON_UNIT`); it only showed on one skeleton seed. Fix: blend half the oriented face normal back in. Lost: about 5 min.
16. **Brief bounds vs the owner's "lower and wider".** Height 11.5 m stays in 11-13 m, but the card-tip canopy extent is 12.3 x 13.6 m, over the brief's 9-12 m, to get ref-1's wide dome. Flagged for the owner.
17. **Canopy underside near black even with up-biased normals.** Raising the foliage up-bias from 0.45 to 0.75 changed nothing visible from below: the dark cards were back faces, and Bevy's double-sided path negates the normal on back faces, turning the authored canopy normal toward the ground. Fix in `kit_material.wesl` and `kit_prepass.wesl`: alpha-masked (foliage) slots shade back faces with the front normal. No binding change; web-smoke still 16/7. Lost: about 10 min.
18. **Unprefixed image datablocks in the owner's file.** `bpy.data.images.load` names an image after its file (`bark.albedo.png`, the HDRI), which broke the owner's prefix rule (O1). Fix: rename after load in `_tree.bark_material` and `live/01_author.py`, renamed live, re-saved; the runtime files stayed byte-identical. Lost: about 5 min.
19. **AO cage sweep (look pass).** At AO distance 0.35 m on the look-pass receiver: cage 0.12 m misses 2.0% of bark texels (mean 0.649), 0.15 m 0.27% (0.676), 0.2 m 0.02% (0.700), 0.25 m 0.02% (0.717). No setting reaches D4's rough 0.75; the shipped 0.25 m has no misses.

## Solid-dome canopy pass (owner)
20. **Baked dome density in one atlas.** Eleven clump shells of about 58 m² each cannot each get unique texels at 256 px/m in a 2048² atlas. Fix: every clump is a transformed copy of one baked R0 shell, and the shell and its leaf-covered master are 3-fold symmetric with every sector on one UV island set: canopy 340 px/m median. The cost is a 120° repeat inside each dome, broken up by per-clump rotation and stretch. Lost: about 10 min of design.
21. **Leaf-only bake source left holes.** The first master dome was only leaves, so the bake rays found nothing in the gaps and wrote 0 (black holes, AO mean 0.63, p05 0). Fix: a dark-green solid dome just under the leaves (0.97 R); canopy AO mean 0.72, p01 0.19, no misses. Lost: about 5 min.
22. **Facets from the normal bake.** Baking tangent normals against the low shell's interpolated normals encoded the low-poly facets, which then showed once the canopy bend replaced those normals. Fix: set the shell's custom normals to the smooth dome's own normals before the bake (`shell_smooth_normals`), and smooth-shade the master's cupped leaves. Lost: about 10 min.
23. **Decimation blends trunk sway into root tips.** LOD2 put U 0.0012 on the lowest vertex and the gate (U ≤ 0.001) failed. Fix: `clamp_wind_uv` snaps U below 0.01 to 0. Lost: about 3 min.
24. **Triangle budget.** Shells at 15 x 6 (180 tris) per clump, 11 clumps, left room for only 847 bark tris and 154 fringe tris (LOD0 2,981 of 3,000). The bark loses faces hidden inside the domes (`cull_inside_crown`, 77) and runs coarser limb rings.

## P2 polish round 2 (headless, criteria 2 and 4)
34. **Rim corners came from latitude spacing, not columns.** The P2 shell rings sat at even latitudes (-0.75, -0.15, 0.35, 0.8, 1.2 rad). On the flattened dome (1.15 R wide, 0.4 R below the equator) the normal turns 48 and 46 degrees across the two bands at the rim, so the silhouette kinks there however many columns it has. Fix: 7 rings at equal 22.5-degree steps of the normal elevation, with 3/4/7/7/7/5/3 columns per sector (fewer near the poles, where a column spans cos(psi) less normal angle). Paid for by roots at a 1.3 m ring step (941 to 887 bark tris) and a 4-column underside ring at psi -45: LOD0 2,956 (bark 887, canopy 1,915, fringe 154), LOD1 1,460, LOD2 432. Folded into `pitfalls.md`. Lost: about 15 min.
35. **The new sector dropped the canopy density to 250 px/m.** Smart project at the lib's 1.152 rad split the tapered sector into less efficient islands. Swept 0.6-1.5 rad: 1.0 gives 258.7 (min 187.5), pinned as `SHELL_UV_ANGLE`. The margin over 256 is thin. Folded into `pitfalls.md`. Lost: about 5 min.
36. **Hidden bark texels cannot be removed within the budget.** An EXACT Boolean self-union of the receiver tubes removes every buried face but takes the bark from 1,144 to 2,254 tris; the MANIFOLD solver does not self-union; trimming each branch's buried start trims nothing, because the roots (r 0.7 at their start) are thicker than the strands they leave (r about 0.45). Area-weighted, 27% of the bark is hidden: trunk 36%, roots 34%, limbs 16%, twigs 10%. So the all-texels AO stays below the visible one. Folded into `pitfalls.md`. Lost: about 15 min.
37. **Fissure-scale ridges on the high poly were AO noise.** The bark high poly feeds only the masks.A AO bake (59 px/m); colour, normal and fissure AO come from the tileable set at 531 px/m. Its 0.07 m ridges did not line up with the set's fissures. `live/07_p3_bark_relief.py` rebuilt only the bark high poly headless with 0.025 m ridges (store revision 25c52ec7; the replaced one kept as `tree_hero_oak_high.adfb21a59601.blend`). Bark AO mean, all texels 0.645 to 0.678; visible texels 0.721 to 0.758 (p05 0.25 to 0.28). The AO distance stays 0.35 m (entry 28). Folded into `pitfalls.md`. Lost: about 5 min.
38. **The default native shots blur the domes.** The kit scenario's tilt-shift (`--tilt auto`) puts the 8 m domes outside its sharp band, which hides rim facets. Rim crops come from a `--tilt off` run (`p3_dome8m-sharp.jpg`, `p3_rims-crop.jpg`). No skill change: `docs/agent-operability.md` documents the flag. Lost: about 3 min.

## Canopy gaps (owner close-up, 2026-10-14)
39. **The shine between the leaves was specular, not albedo.** The dark dome under the baked leaves was already 5.8x darker than the leaves (linear luma 0.032 vs 0.183), but the shell's ORM.G was the flat 0.62 fill and its normal flat, so F0 0.04 reflected the sky over a near-black albedo: on screen the gaps measured (75, 80, 73) at saturation 0.11 on the shade side. Fix: the generator masks the gap texels from the baked albedo (`GAP_*`, leaf texels byte-identical), takes 0.5x linear albedo and roughness 1.0 there; at roughness 1 the bake-only shots still kept a grey haze on the shaded domes (saturation 0.25), so `kit_material.wesl` fades F0 to 0 on unique foliage above roughness 0.95 (no other foliage slot reaches it). Evidence and numbers: `docs/checkpoints/oak-canopy/`. Folded into `pitfalls.md`. Lost: about 10 min.

## Trunk and size pass (owner trunk close-up, 2026-10-14)
40. **The collar was the eight root start rings.** Every root started 0.5 m off the axis at z 1.6 m with radius 0.7, while the strand bundle there is about 0.95 m across, so the eight start rings stood about 0.25 m proud with their up-facing caps: a ring of lit ledges at 1.6-2.0 m (the band with a lighter rim). Fix: each root starts on the centreline of the strand nearest its heading, thinner than that strand's inscribed receiver tube (0.32 m inside 0.38 m), so `cull_buried` removes its start ring and cap; start heights spread per root over 1.0-1.8 m (a first try at one height left a new ring of root tops at the exit height), and an outward power of 1.5 makes the root run down along its strand before it turns out, a concave flare. The rng draws are unchanged, so the limbs, the leaf and shell wind and the canopy stay as approved (pinned by `LIMBS_SHA`). The roots are still separate tubes: each meets its strand along a diagonal intersection crease (shaded across by `junction_normals`, with a bark UV break there); a welded or swept flare was not built (an EXACT union doubled the bark, entry 36, and the LOD0 budget had 44 tris spare). Folded into `pitfalls.md`. Lost: about 10 min.
41. **The bark high poly lived in the .blend.** New roots need a matching bake source, so the generator now builds the bark high poly from the current skeleton (smooth: the tileable set carries the fissures, and the P3 ridges were z-elongated world noise that baked vertical AO streaks); the .blend still supplies the canopy master and the leaf cards, so its revision (25c52ec7) is unchanged and nothing was written to the shared store. No skill change (piece-specific). Lost: about 3 min.

Folded into `pitfalls.md`. Lost: about 8 min.
