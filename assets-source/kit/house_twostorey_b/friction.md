Members are ranked by proud (bands 6 cm, posts 5, studs 4, braces 3.5) and posts and studs run band-centre to band-centre, so no two overlapping members share a face plane. Minutes lost: 0 (planned before the first build). Skill: no change (the house family's own convention, documented in `_house_jettied.py`).
