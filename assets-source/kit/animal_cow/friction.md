# animal_cow friction (P3, shared with animal_sheep through `kit/_animals.py`)

1. **Loft part ends poked out of their host part.** The first build put the back thigh's top ring, the ear roots and the tail root outside the body or head, which leaves visible holes and open rims. Fix: `_animals.check_buried(SPEC, LODS)` tests every open ring end against its host part's superellipse field at every LOD's ring count before anything is built. It caught three more such misses during tuning. Minutes lost: about 5. Proposed skill text below (P1).
2. **The head's underside showed through the neck as a thin white line** in the textured still: the white jaw paint ran back along the head loft past where the neck covers it, so the seam between two overlapping lofts drew a light sliver. Fix: limit the jaw paint to the front of the head (`sf > 0.5`). Minutes lost: about 5. No skill change: it is specific to two lofts overlapping with contrasting paint.
3. **A graze-weight field along a single path tears the neck root.** The first `Motion` sent the head weight along the neck-and-head path from the neck pivot, so V was already above 0 where the neck leaves the body and a pitch would have opened the shoulder. Fix: V is a position field along the pivot-to-muzzle axis, zero on the plane through `NECK_BASE` (where the neck leaves the chest), with the rotation pivot set separately inside the shoulder. Ears take the value at their root. Minutes lost: about 10, at design time. Proposed skill text below (P2).
4. **`hidden()` and `local()` divided by zero on flat caps.** A hoof sole is a pole at its ring's own centre, so the path segment has length 0. Fix: skip zero-length segments. Minutes lost: 2. No skill change needed.
5. **The worktree hook refused an empty heredoc probe** (`python3 - <<'EOF'`), as SKILL.md says it will. Minutes lost: 1. No change: already in the skill.

6. **LOD2 found after the gates (open, not rebuilt).** A textured still of LOD2 (`still.py -- animal_cow <out> 2`) shows the head as a beak (pole, one ring, pole at 5 sides) and AO-black texels at the leg tops: LOD2's 8-sided body, built from fewer stations, sits inside LOD0's surface, so leg-top texels that LOD0 buries (AO 0) show. `check_buried` tests against the analytic host through all stations, so it cannot see this. LOD2 draws only beyond 400 m (a few pixels), so it is left for the next rebuild: head stations [0, 2, 5, 7] at LOD2 (+10 tris, 158 of the 158.7 ceiling), and fill hidden texels' AO from their visible neighbours. Minutes lost: 3 (found by the end check).

7. **lod2-neck-fix (2026-10-20, every `_animals` piece; closes note 6's AO half, animal_cow_b note 4 and animal_sheep_c note 4).** Cause, measured per piece on outside view hits (`_animals.lod_visible_ao`, bake report `bakeStats.lodVisibleAo`): about a third of each atlas (0.28-0.34 of island texels) is buried inside another part at LOD0 and baked to AO 0, and LOD1/LOD2's inscribed polygons leave part of that band outside their coarser host. Visible hits below AO 0.25 on the committed maps, LOD0 / LOD1 / LOD2: animal_cow 0.05 / 1.7 / 3.2%, animal_cow_b 0.06 / 1.8 / 2.5%, animal_sheep 0.04 / 3.2 / 3.5%, animal_sheep_b 0.07 / 3.4 / 3.4%, animal_sheep_c 0.03 / 2.0 / 3.7%; on the LOD2 neck 10.3, 5.2, 8.3, 18.5 and 3.6%, the LOD1 ears 18-25%. 59-84% of the dark LOD2 hits sit on texels `hidden()` flags. Fix: `fill_hidden_ao` gives every buried texel (plus texels just inside another part's analytic surface, and the junction crease within 12 px, that bake below 0.2; never the tail, which no LOD2 draws) the AO of the nearest texel of its own island at least 6 px from the buried set, and only ever brightens a texel; every other texel keeps its bake bit for bit. After, against the raw bake at 1024 px: LOD2 neck 0.00-0.06% dark, legs 0.00%, all hits 0.5-1.7%; at LOD0 and LOD1 every part group's p05 and mean are equal or higher than the raw bake's (LOD0 all hits 0.00-0.01% dark). The remaining LOD2 dark hits are on the heads (1.4-6.9%: the coarse LOD2 head samples LOD0's own crease texels across open surface, and animal_cow's LOD2 head is still the beak of note 6; the sheep heads are black-faced). Minutes lost: about 15 (a pilot rebuild to widen the mask; a NaN from the median fallback on animal_sheep_c's fully buried tail island; a third rebuild after the per-group numbers showed `hidden()` flags a few texels LOD0 does draw, which a grown value darkened: animal_cow's tail p05 0.56 to 0.44, animal_sheep_c's tail flattened to 1.0, hence brighten-only and no tail). Proposed skill text below (P3).

## Proposed skill text (for the orchestrator to fold in)
- **P3, pitfalls.md (Cycles bakes):** "A unique AO bake on LOD0 leaves every texel LOD0 buries (a loft's root inside its host, about a third of an animal atlas) at 0. Coarser LODs that rebuild the same analytic UV from fewer stations sit inside LOD0's surface and expose part of that band, so they draw black patches at the junctions. Fill the buried texels from the visible AO of their own island, seeded a few texels away from the buried set (`_animals.fill_hidden_ao`), and measure what each LOD shows by casting view rays at every LOD and sampling the map at the hits (`_animals.lod_visible_ao`), not only at LOD0."
- **P1, pitfalls.md (Leaves, foliage and LODs, or a new "Creatures" list):** "Loft-built creatures (`kit/_animals.py`): check every open loft end against its host part's field at every LOD's ring count (`check_buried`), not by eye. A thigh ring 1.5% outside the body is invisible in the six-view and opens a hole in play. Test against each LOD's own host stations, not the analytic host, and render a textured still of LOD1 and LOD2 too: a coarse body sits inside LOD0's surface and shows the AO-black texels LOD0 buries."
- **P2, project.md (beside the tree wind data):** "Farm animal motion data (TEXCOORD_1, `motion` UV map, every LOD primitive; contract in `animal_cow.py`): U = leg weight, (hipY - y) / hipY on a leg and 0 elsewhere, with every leg vertex below hipY; V = the leg code (0.125/0.375/0.625/0.875 FL/FR/BL/BR) where U > 0, else the head weight, a position field along neckPivot -> muzzle tip that is 0 on the plane through neckBase and 1 at the tip (ears take their root's value). A prop carrying TEXCOORD_1 passes `kit_gate` with no gate branch as long as its slot has no `wind` amplitude, so never put `wind` on an animal slot (the foliage sway would bend it). `_animals.check_motion` re-reads it from the exported GLB. The head weight is a ramp along the whole neck and head, so a shader that pitches by theta x V bends the head itself (the cow's poll sits at V 0.31, the muzzle at 1). Remap it to w = clamp(V / vPoll, 0, 1) (extras `vPoll`; the back of the skull reaches a little lower) so the head turns rigidly and only the neck bends, or saturate V over the head at the next rebuild. The graze pivot sits inside the shoulder, not at the neck base: from the neck base the cow's 0.66 m muzzle arm cannot reach the ground 1.12 m below. `gaitPhase` pairs the diagonals, which is a trot; a four-beat walk needs quarter phases (FL 0, BR 0.25, FR 0.5, BL 0.75)."
