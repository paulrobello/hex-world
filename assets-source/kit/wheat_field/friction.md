# wheat_field friction log

1. **A tile's centre piece is walk and scatter ground.** `scatter::ground_pieces` puts the centre into the tile's `Surface` (LOD0 triangles, topmost wins), which the walker, the scatter masks and the grass all stand on. Any wheat card with a ground footprint would lift the walker onto the canopy (or block it as a step over `MAX_STEP`) and put grass blades on the cards. Only exactly vertical triangles are dropped (`Surface::new` skips xz-degenerate ones). Fix: the wheat slot is kept out of the ground by name (`scatter::NOT_GROUND`, tested on the real GLB), so the cards may lean. Minutes lost: 5 (reading the ground path). Skill: proposed `project.md` "Fast loops and runtime couplings" line: "A tile's `center` piece is ground: its LOD0 triangles join the walk, scatter and grass `Surface`. Plant cards on a centre piece (a crop) must be listed in `scatter::NOT_GROUND`, or the walker stands on them."
2. **A horizontal top card reads as a dark ledge.** The first build carried a near-horizontal "ear canopy" card over each row for the top-down camera; in the EEVEE still every row showed a dark shelf along its top (the card's alpha-tested gap tone, lit flat). Fix: a V pair of row cards leaning out 22 degrees plus a vertical centre card on LOD0; the tile's ear band is a solid golden crown, so the leaning cards read gold from above. Minutes lost: 6. Skill: proposed `pitfalls.md` line under "Leaves, foliage and LODs": "Crop rows: lean the row cards outward (a V pair, about 20 degrees) instead of a horizontal top card; a flat card over a row reads as a ledge."
3. **CDT slivers where constraint lines end on the outer polygon.** Furrow lines ending exactly on the field hexagon's edges left collinear triangles, which the export's unit-vector check rejected (zero tangents). Fix: `earth_lod` drops faces with near-zero 2D area and reindexes. Minutes lost: 2. Skill: no change (the export check named the mesh and primitive, as designed).
4. **Coverage per mip at the deepest levels.** A 341-row tile reaches 2-5 rows at its last mips, where a single row decides (the first draft's top tile read 0.33 at mip 7). Fix: the generator asserts coverage at or above 0.5 for every mip at least 8 px tall; the row tiles measure 0.94-0.97 down the chain. Minutes lost: 1. Skill: proposed `gates.md` "Alpha coverage" note: "measure down to mips at least 8 px tall per tile".
6. **Dark vertical lines where row cards butt.** Every card joint showed a dark slit in engine and in the EEVEE close still. Cause: `_wheat.row_tile` stamps each stalk with wrapped columns (so the tile wraps), but took the stem and ear normal tilt from the wrapped column minus the unwrapped stalk centre, so a stalk across u = 0/1 got a tilt of about ±1000 px / half-width; the 2x2 box downsample to the 512² normal map averaged it into a fully sideways normal at exactly the columns every joint samples (wrap columns 0.19-0.23 mean |R - 0.5| against a 0.048 median). Fix: `_wrap_dx` brings the delta into [-w/2, w/2) at both call sites (wrap columns 0.044-0.056 after). Minutes lost: 10. Skill: proposed `pitfalls.md` line under "Leaves, foliage and LODs": "A horizontally wrapping card tile (crop rows) must take per-pixel geometry (normal tilt, distance) from a wrap-aware delta, not from the wrapped column minus an unwrapped centre: the seam texels then carry a huge tilt that the downsample turns into a dark line at every joint. Check the normal map's per-column mean tilt at the wrap columns."
5. **The worktree hook refused a heredoc and `python3 -`.** Fix: script files under the scratchpad, as SKILL.md says. Minutes lost: 1. Skill: no change.
