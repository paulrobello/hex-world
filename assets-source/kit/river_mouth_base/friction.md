# river_mouth_base friction (2026-10-14)

Shared with `river_mouth_l_base` (one helper, `scripts/blender/kit/_mouth.py`).

1. **Row s = -8 runs through the stub's notch.** The coast row from corner 2 toward corner 4 crosses the notch side (y = -6.83) at x = -13.18, outside the cap. Every row point, row segment, lattice seed and polyline point is clipped to the domain (hex minus notch) with a margin, so nothing lands on a boundary edge (a CDT Steiner vertex there would change the ring). About 3 minutes.
2. **Joint row versus mouth profile.** river_edge's joint section (`_river.profile_z`) and `_mouth_profile.height` differ by 1.7 mm at the joint row's y = -6.83 end (s = -7.72, where the coast term begins). The joint keeps river_edge's exact values: it is not on the hex border, and the profile check still passes there. About 2 minutes. Skill note: none needed, the PLAN's "s <= -8 on the whole River edge" holds on the border itself.
3. **Constant-x cross-sections miss part of the channel region.** On the inland side the in-channel region runs past the centre line's tip (s = 3 at y = 0, while (3.59, 2.89) is still in the channel), so a crease found on lines of constant x was cut short and joined to the tip by a long chord. Refinement could not fix an error that lay on the chord, and looped. The fix parameterises everything along iso-s rows, where the in-channel set is one interval about the centre line that ends at s = 3. About 6 minutes. Candidate pitfall (water beds): "a min() bed's crease belongs to the coordinate in which the coast is constant".
4. **Greedy Delaunay refinement is isotropic and costs about 3x the budget.** Worst-sample insertion alone gave LOD2 1,092 tris. Lines at fixed d / w(s) (the joint offsets) as constraints give long triangles along the channel and brought LOD2 to about 700. The PLAN's 400 is not reached at the contract tolerance: the ring plus skirt fix about 200 and the fan's across-channel lines and crease about 500. The 20% lane-cut ceiling holds (709 of 3,592). About 8 minutes.
5. **Zero tangents (gltf-validator ACCESSOR_VECTOR3_NON_UNIT in kit-compress).** Two causes: plan slivers from points within millimetres of a constraint (fixed by dropping points closer than 3 cm to another point or a constraint, the crease taking priority, and inserting only interior samples), and the hull collider, which mixes cap and constant-UV skirt vertices and has near-collinear wall faces (fixed by dissolving degenerate hull faces, flat shading, and an oblique planar UV on the collider). About 6 minutes. Candidate pitfall (export): "a hull collider over a constant-UV skirt can carry zero tangents".
6. **A fanned skirt wall made a near-zero-area triangle that read as an up-facing bed face.** Fanning the one-row skirt from the ocean corner (s = 8, at the base) put that corner and the beach-edge ring points at s = 4 and 4.5 almost on one 3D line (the drop is nearly straight there). The triangle's normal was noise, so an independent N = 4 sampler of the exported GLB (the gate's density) counted it as bed and found 0.27 m off. The fan now starts under the higher end of each run, and every skirt face is asserted over 1e-3 m². About 4 minutes. The in-memory profile check covered only the cap: check the exported GLB as the gate does.
7. **Shading sawtooth along the crease.** A vertex normal averaged across the crease, and crease points dropped by the sliver filter, gave a toothed line in the top render. Per-loop normals from the face's own side (channel gradient or the coast's analytic normal) and crease priority in the filter fixed it. About 4 minutes.
8. **The fan's bed colour ended in a crisp line (owner, 2026-10-14).** Per-face slots cannot blend, and the gravel bed met plaster sand at the crease: a gravel tint raised to the sand's mean puts bright stones over albedo 1 (gravel's linear std is about 0.13 against plaster's 0.03). The fix keeps gravel under the water (it meets the gravel seabed) and on the dark core, puts the banks between that seam and the crease on the coast's plaster slots by height (so they take the live wet band), and writes COLOR_0 into the GLB per primitive: rgb darkens each slot toward one target colour field, a fades the set to its top-mip mean, its relief and the band (`kit_material.wesl`). About 40 minutes, most of it choosing the mechanism. Folded into pitfalls (water beds).
