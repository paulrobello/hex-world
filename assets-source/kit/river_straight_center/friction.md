## Close-up reeds and cattails (2026-10-14)

Owner: "cat tails need more close up detail" (flat blade triangles and flat brown cattail cards, seen close to a bank; a first-person walk mode at 1.7 m eye height is landing). `_river_dress._reed_clump` and `_cattail` now build curved, twisted, tapered strap blades with a shallow V (folded midrib) on the upper side and a flat back sheet, and closed cattails (3-sided stalk, 6-sided head, pale 3-sided spike).

1. **The kind budget sets the clump, not the look** (about 20 min). Tris LOD0/1/2 went from 860/320/120 to 1473/320/120 (prop, 1,500 LOD0 budget, unchanged). On the props that budget leaves about 90 tris per clump, so a clump is one bending-over V blade (21 tris), one tall V blade (15), five to nine flat two-segment blades (6 each) and at most one cattail (31: stalk 6, head 22, spike 3), down from nine flat blades and three cards; every count depends on the seed and the LOD only, so the mirrored mouth pieces keep equal counts. A V on both sides or 8-sided heads would have needed a per-piece budget above the kind's. LOD1 keeps four flat back-to-back triangles per clump (now along four LOD0 blades) and LOD2 no reeds, so their counts are unchanged.
