# ocean_base friction

- **The gate's base ring assumed y = 0.** `kit_gate::border_ring` kept only border vertices at y = 0, so a base whose cap ring sits at the ocean bed (y = -4) reported "border ring has 0 vertices", and the base cap-normal check looked only at y = 0. Fix: `base_ring_level` reads the up-facing border vertices of a base's LOD0, requires them flat and at a contract level (`BASE_RING_LEVELS`: 0 for Grass/Forest, -LEVEL_STEP for Ocean), and the ring and normal checks run at that level; test `base_ring_level_must_be_flat_and_a_contract_level`. About 10 minutes. Folded into `project.md` (Seams).
- **Lib border helpers assume z = 0.** `cdt_cap`, `set_border_normals_up` and `lod.assert_border_identical` all take the ring at z = 0, so each LOD is built at z = 0 and its vertex data then dropped by the bed level (a node transform would not do: the gate reads raw accessor positions). No time lost; folded into `project.md` (Seams).
- **The contract tightened mid-build** (W3c 176f1e7): the ocean floor's allowance fell from +0.25 m to +0.10 m and the profiles became requirements. Relief cut to 0.06 m plus 0.03 m ripples, and the generator now asserts every LOD (and the high-poly) against the profile with `_coast.bed_profile_errors`. One rebuild, about 5 minutes.
- **AO from gentle ripples is nearly flat.** Ripples of 3 cm over 0.85 m give almost no occlusion at a 0.6 m AO distance (masks.A mean 0.998, p05 0.984); only the stone contact rings register. That is physically right and the bed has no unique normal slot (the gravel set holds it), so no fake ripple shading was added. No change needed.
