Fix: none in code. Proposed skill text for `references/dispatch.md`: "For a new network family (rail, canal), the orchestrator pastes the gate branches the piece will hit (center_lanes fallback, lane_joint_errors by extras.kind) into the brief, so the agent does not re-derive them from kit_gate.rs."

Proposed SKILL.md addition under "A new kind of piece needs its gate branch first": "a centre with lane sockets that no tile names yet is checked at edges 0 and 3 only."
