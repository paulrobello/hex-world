# beach_base friction (shared by the six beach pieces)

1. **The lib's border helpers assume a flat ring at z = 0** (about 10 min, design). `geom.cdt_cap`, `geom.set_border_normals_up` and `lod.assert_border_identical` filter on z = 0, and a beach ring follows the coast profile on its beach edges. `_beach.py` builds its own constrained-Delaunay cap (rows constrained), compares rings on (x, y, z) and sets analytic normals. Skill: `pitfalls.md` (Water beds).
2. **The 1 m ring lattice misses the smoothstep drop** (5 min). Linear interpolation between integer s on the drop is 0.054 m off the profile, over the 0.03 m tolerance even on the border. Every coast base carries extra beach-edge samples at s = 3.5-7.5 (and the damp band's top at -1.953); the gate checks they are identical on all coast bases. Skill: `pitfalls.md`, `gates.md`.
3. **Arc LOD2 over 15%** (15 min, controller-sanctioned extension). The corner tiles' arc rows on the drop (slope up to 0.67) need chord sag x slope inside what the row spacing leaves of 0.03 m, so LOD2 is 562 / 590 tris against LOD0 3,144 / 3,246 (17.9%, 18.2%); padding LOD0 to 3,950 to reach 15% would only game the ratio. The corner bases take the lane-cut 20% ceiling (`kit_gate` `COAST_ARC_LOD2_MAX_RATIO`); the straight beach stays at 7.9%. Skill: `gates.md` (LOD floor).
4. **A tileable slot cannot be glossier** (3 min). `kit_material.wesl` replaces a tileable slot's roughness with ORM.G, so the damp band is a darker tint of the plaster set on its own slot, not a lower roughness. Skill: `pitfalls.md`.
5. **Damp band too thin at the placeholder's height** (5 min, look). With the band ending at y = -1.0 (s = -1.24) the static band is 1.2 m wide and the live swash covers half of it; the refs show a clear band, so it ends at y = -0.8 (s = -1.95). Contract §3 updated.
6. **Worktree hook refuses `for` loops and inline heredoc generators** (5 min). Generator files and multi-piece runs went into scratch scripts run with one plain `bash`/`python3` command, as the skill's worktree rule says. No skill change needed.
