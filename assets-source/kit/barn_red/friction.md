# barn_red friction log

1. **The masks size in the brief and in `project.md` (512²) disagrees with the shipped houses (1024²).** `project.md` "Budgets" says a house bakes "only a unique 512² `masks`", but `_house.MASK_SIZE` is 1024 and the approved cottage's bake report records 1024 at 15-18 px/m. The barn has about 2.5x the cottage's visible surface, so measured on the skip-bake build its masks density is 12.9 px/m at 1024² on every LOD and would be 6.4 px/m at 512², half the approved family's. Fix: the barn ships a 1024² masks (bc7 254,886 B, astc 242,691 B against the 1.5 MB `piece` budget). Minutes lost: 3. Skill: proposed `project.md` "Budgets" text: "bakes only a unique `masks` (1024² for the shipped houses and the barn, about 13-18 px/m AO; AO, plus night B on the glass)".
2. **`_house.py` could not be imported this batch.** The bakery agent owns `_house.py` in the same batch and may edit it; importing it would put its hash in barn_red's `generatorDeps` and stale the barn as soon as the bakery lands. The barn also runs its ridge along x (doors on the gable), which `_house.build` does not do. Fix: `barn_red.py` is self-contained (its own slotted bmesh accumulator and roof-course profile, written for the gable-front layout). Minutes lost: 0. Skill: no change; a later `_farm.py` can take the shared parts when the farm family grows.
3. **Hidden faces dilute masks density.** `_house` builds a plinth box with a top face under the whole floor, gable end caps behind the bargeboards and a wall behind the door leaves. On the barn those would be about 70 + 2 x 6 + 10 m² of atlas nobody sees. Fix: the plinth is its four sides and a 5 cm ledge, LOD0/1 have bargeboards in place of end caps, the door opening is cut out of the front gable wall, and the soffits are built only under the overhangs. The plinth's front face still runs behind the door leaves (about 1 m², baked A < 0.05, unseen). Minutes lost: 0. Skill: proposed `pitfalls.md` line under "Cycles bakes": "A unique masks bake spends texels on every face, seen or not: leave out faces nobody can see (plinth tops under a floor, wall behind a door leaf, end caps behind bargeboards, soffit over the interior) before the unwrap."
4. **Sub-texel strips bake nothing and sample A = 0.** At 12.9 px/m the batten sides (2.5 cm), slate-course butts (3 cm), the 5 cm plinth ledge and the frame sides cover no texel centre, so Cycles and its margin skip their islands entirely: 382 LOD0 triangles (20.5 m², 5%) sampled G = 0 and A = 0 at their UV centroid, so they would draw black and take no snow in engine. The hooks gate passed anyway, because it checks each slot's maximum, not every face. Fix: `fill_unbaked` in `barn_red.py` rasterises every triangle conservatively (0.75 px reach) after the bake and writes its slot's hooks and median baked AO into unbaked texels only, then grows 4 px (inside the 12 px island spacing) so the mips hold. Measured after: 0 triangles on unbaked texels on every LOD. Minutes lost: 8. Skill: proposed `pitfalls.md` line under "Cycles bakes": "At house-scale masks densities (13-18 px/m) any face narrower than about 8 cm bakes no texel: sample masks at every LOD face's UV centroid on the reimported GLB, and fill unbaked texels under faces with the slot's hooks and AO (`barn_red.fill_unbaked`). The hooks gate checks a slot's maximum, so it does not catch this." Measured on the approved house_cottage with the same script: 74 of 1,048 LOD0 triangles (4.4 m²) and 22 of 436 LOD1 triangles (1.0 m²) sit on unbaked texels (A = 0, R = 0), so the shipped houses carry the same defect.
5. **No committed tool binds world-space tileable slots for a textured Blender still.** `docs/checkpoints/prop-variants/lineup.py` binds a tileable set on UV0 at `tileUvScale` (right for bark only); the houses' and the barn's plaster slots are world-space and UV0 is the masks atlas. Fix: `docs/checkpoints/barn_red/still.py` box-projects the set on object coordinates at 1 / tileM, multiplies by the slot tint and by masks.A on UV0, culls back faces (as the kit does), and lights with `KIT_SUN_DIR`. Minutes lost: 5. Skill: proposed `pitfalls.md` addition to "The 6-view render is untextured": "for world-space tileable slots (houses, barn) use `docs/checkpoints/barn_red/still.py`, which box-projects the set and binds masks.A."
6. **The worktree hook refused a `for n in …; do magick … $n …` PNG-mean loop.** Fix: the shot wrapper `run.sh` (a script file, run as one command) already checks every PNG's mean; read its exit code instead. Minutes lost: 1. Skill: no change (SKILL.md "Worktree sessions" already says loops with variables are refused).
7. **Shade sides read near-black in engine.** The back gable at 9 m is almost black under the default grade (rich-3, ACES fitted). The asset's albedo is the main cause, with ACES on top (the Q4 ruling: plain ACES clips shaded walls): the barn wall is tint x plaster = linear (0.30, 0.046, 0.030), luminance about 0.10, in the band of the houses' dark timber and slate, which also go near-black in the same frames, while the houses' cream plaster (luminance about 0.63) does not. The masks AO on that wall is 0.85-1.0, so the bake does not darken it. Fix: none applied; the owner chooses between keeping the red or lifting its value (luminance about 0.13-0.15, still clearly red) and re-shooting (`docs/review/2026-10-19-barn_red-pending.md`). Minutes lost: 0. Skill: proposed `pitfalls.md` line under "Look-dev and engine shots": "A dark tinted wall (linear luminance about 0.1 or less, barn red, slate, dark timber) goes near-black on the shade side under the default ACES grade; shoot the shade side before settling a saturated dark tint."
8. **A 2.5 cm batten side seen edge-on aliases into a dashed line at eye level.** Sub-pixel geometry, visible only where the camera grazes a batten side. Minutes lost: 0. Skill: no change; the options are left to the owner (wider 4 cm battens, or accept it).
