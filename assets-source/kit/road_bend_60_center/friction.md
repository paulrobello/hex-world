# road_bend_60_center friction

1. **The arc is tighter than the profile** (about 20 min of design). The stubs fix the arc radius at 6 tan 30 deg = 3.464 m, below the profile's 4.6 m half width, so the inner verge points (t = -4, -4.6) fall past the arc centre C, onto the far side of both joint lines. The joint gate still needs them in the section. Fix in `_road_bend.bend_mesh`: sweep down to the kerb foot (0.21 m from C), close the pit with a fan at VERGE_TOP, and sweep the two dropped points round the far side of C with their own fan. The fan apexes sit 2 mm from C so no vertex lands on a joint plane; the gap between them is under 2 mm. Skill: `pitfalls.md` (Turning road pieces).
2. **The two frozen edge stubs overlap at the inner corner** (the visible crease is resolved; corrected 2026-10-21). Edge 0 and edge 1 `road_straight_edge` stubs share the wedge between them: their verge tops are coplanar there, and each stub's sink slope crosses the other's verge. The darker line in the 2026-10-13 shots (`shots/engine-overview.jpg`, `shots/engine-joint-edge1.jpg`) was not the overlap itself: it was one stub's inner verge sink slope on its true normal, facing away from the sun, cut off diagonally by the other stub's verge top, so it read as a crease. 69f7f6f (2026-10-14, +Z normals on every road_verge loop) removed it; swapping only `road_straight_edge.glb` back to 69f7f6f^ brings it back, swapping only this piece's GLB back does not, and HEAD at forced LOD 1 and 2 shows no z-fight where the verge tops still overlap (`docs/checkpoints/road_bend_60-crease/sheet.jpg`). The overlap remains in the geometry; trimming it would need two mirrored tile-specific stubs, a matching inner side on this piece and a kit gate change (the joint check compares every road centre with every road edge piece), so it is left as an owner question.
3. **Self-review composite needed an XYZ rotation mode** (3 min). The glTF importer leaves imported roots in quaternion mode, so setting `rotation_euler` to place stubs at other edges did nothing. Set `rotation_mode = "XYZ"` first. Added to `pitfalls.md` (Coordinate frame and export).
4. **The crease card was stale by the time it was worked** (about 15 min, 2026-10-21). The card was filed on 2026-10-13 from shots taken before 69f7f6f, and the fix brief proposed new stub pieces. Shooting the corner at HEAD first showed no crease; rebuilding the 2026-10-13 state from the git GLBs in a scratch `assets/` copy (`docs/checkpoints/road_bend_60-crease/prefix.sh`, which runs the shot runner from that copy because native runs load `<cwd>/assets`) and swapping one GLB at a time pinned the cause in two short runs. Proposed skill text (`pitfalls.md`, Turning road pieces): "**Re-shoot an old visual card at HEAD before fixing it.** A look card can be fixed by a later change to a shared helper (the 60-degree bend crease was the verge sink normals fixed by 69f7f6f a day after it was filed). Shoot the card's view at HEAD first; for the before side, rebuild the filed state from the git GLBs in a scratch `assets/` copy (`git archive <rev> <glb>...`, run the binary or `scripts/shots/piece.py` with that copy as the working directory) and swap one GLB at a time to find the piece that carries the defect (`docs/checkpoints/road_bend_60-crease/prefix.sh`). Native runs have no asset-root flag, so `make shots-piece` cannot do this from the repo root."
5. **The overlap trimmed** (owner A7, 2026-10-25). Item 2's open question, done: the tile now places `road_bend_60_edge_e0` and `road_bend_60_edge_e1` (road_straight_edge cut at the inner bisector, e1 the mirror of e0) through `edge_pieces: Explicit(...)`, this piece drops the far-side fan and sink rows (they lay entirely under both stubs) and its pit fan's apex moves from 2 mm off the arc centre C to C itself, which both trimmed joint sections now contain. New code lives in `_road_bend_trim.py` (module-global overrides of `_road_bend.bend_mesh` and `joint_section_errors`), so `_road_bend.py` and the pieces that import it stay current.
