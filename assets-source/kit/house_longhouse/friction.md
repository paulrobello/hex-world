# house_longhouse friction log

1. **A lower range dying into a taller cross wing needs its roof ended on the wing's slope plane.** The bakery's cross wing is the smaller roof and dies into the main one, so a constant sweep end works there. Here the hall is lower, and a constant end would leave its eave slab standing proud in front of the wing's gable. Fix: `_house_longhouse.sweep` takes one end position per profile vertex, and each hall vertex ends `OVERLAP` (0.08 m) inside the wing's -y slope (`y = wing eave y + (z - wing eave z) / tan(wing pitch) + OVERLAP`). The edges of each quad stay parallel to y, so the quads stay planar. The first top and back stills showed a clean valley. Minutes lost: 0 (planned before the first build). Skill: proposed `pitfalls.md` text under "Coordinate frame and export" or a new "Buildings" list: "Where a lower roof dies into a taller one, end each of the lower roof's profile vertices on the taller roof's slope plane, a few cm inside its slab (`_house_longhouse.sweep` per-vertex ends). A constant sweep end leaves the lower eave standing proud of the taller gable."
2. **A wing verge that overhangs past the front wall hangs over the lower range.** At a 0.25 m wing overhang, the -y verge and its bargeboard came down about 0.2 m below the hall's eave, over the door frame, in the first front still. Fix: wing overhang 0.15, and the -y bargeboard starts at the hall's eave line. Minutes lost: 5. Skill: no change (specific to this plan).
3. **An L-plan building needs a box collider, not the family's hull.** `_house.build_piece` builds a convex hull collider. An L's hull fills about 90% of its bounds, and `scatter::ground_boxes_match_the_kit` requires 95% for a `GROUND_BOXES` entry. Fix: `collider_from(lods[2], "box", ...)` in the variant's own `build_piece`. Minutes lost: 0. Skill: proposed `project.md` Buildings text: "A non-rectangular building (an L plan) takes a `box` collider: the grass clear box and the walker need a rectangle that the hull of an L does not fill (`ground_boxes_match_the_kit`, 95%)."
4. **The family's approved helper could not take the new parts.** The brief forbids editing `_house.py`, so the L-plan builder, the per-vertex roof sweep, the brick slot and the box collider live in `_house_longhouse.py`. That file imports `_house`'s `Mesh`, `Wall`, `roof_profile`, `fill_unbaked` and `centroid_samples`, plus copies of the bakery's `box`/`beam` (the bakery keeps them private to its generator). Minutes lost: 0. Skill: no change (SKILL.md "Helpers" already says this).
