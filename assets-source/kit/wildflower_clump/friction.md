# wildflower_clump friction log

1. **The piece KTX2 budget was 99% full.** With four 1024² maps the clump measured 1,475,944 B BC7 and 1,485,935 B ASTC, against a 1,500,000 B limit. The normal map alone took 0.59 MB. The fix ships the normal at 512² (a 2×2 average, renormalized), which brought the clump to 1,066,094 B BC7 and 1,078,566 B ASTC. The albedo stays at 1024² because it carries the alpha edges and sets the density the gate measures. Lost about 5 minutes. Skill: the Props paragraph in `project.md` now says maps may differ in size.
2. **Alpha coverage of grass tufts sits near 0.5.** The first side-view tufts measured about 0.40 and the top-view rosettes 0.24. I made the blades denser and wider and the rosettes larger, which gave 0.46-0.53 for the tufts and 0.35 for the rosettes, whose footprint is a disc and caps them at about 0.6. Every tile holds its coverage within ±0.05 down to the 4×4 mip, because the masks are clumped rather than thin. Lost about 5 minutes. Skill: a `pitfalls.md` note under "Leaves, foliage and LODs".
3. **Flat rosette cards streaked at a low camera.** The near-horizontal flower cards read as smeared horizontal streaks from about 1 m up. The fix tilts each card outward along the clump's dome and drops it lower inside the tufts. Lost about 5 minutes. Skill: a `pitfalls.md` note under "Leaves, foliage and LODs".
4. **`kit-compress` exited 0 on a stage without a GLB.** A `kit-build` failed on the wind self-check after the PNGs were written but before the export. The `kit-compress` that ran after it in the same command printed `glb - B` and exited 0. A rebuild repaired it, and the committed files come from a complete stage. This is an upstream issue in `scripts/assets/kit-compress.mjs`, which is outside this card's paths. It is reported to the controller and not fixed here. Lost about 2 minutes.
5. **Wind U was not 0 below the ground on LOD2.** LOD2's larger rosette cards, once tilted, dipped below z = 0 while still carrying U = 0.7. The generator's self-check caught it, and the fix sets U = 0 on any corner at or below the ground. No skill change: the tree wind contract already says U = 0 at the lowest vertices.
