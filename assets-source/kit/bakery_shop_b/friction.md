# bakery_shop_b friction log

1. **The family pipeline does not match bakery_shop's bake settings.** `bakery_shop.py` is monolithic (it runs `main()` on import) and its own pipeline differs from `_house_longhouse.build_piece` in five ways: island margin 0.006 with a FRACTION pack and a 2 px bake margin (the family: 0.012 and 4 px), `LOD_UV_SCALE` (LOD0 gets about 70% of the atlas), `DISPLAY_BOOST` on the shop panes, the authored loaf field in masks A/B, and a hull collider. Driving the family pipeline unchanged would have dropped the walls toward the family's 13-15 px/m and lost the night loaves. Fix: `_bakery_b.build_piece` sets seven of `lh`'s module globals: the two margins, `build`, the two fit bounds, `uv` (a `SimpleNamespace` whose `atlas_unwrap` is bakery_shop's unwrap, re-derived) and `fill_unbaked` (writes the loaves over LOD0's shop-pane texels, then calls `_house.fill_unbaked`), because `build_piece` looks every one of them up in its own namespace at call time. Result: walls 23.25 px/m at LOD0 (bakery_shop 20.7), shop panes 58.1, 0 unbaked triangles, and no approved helper or generator edited. The collider stays the family's LOD2 box (it fills 100% of its bounds, which `ground_boxes_match_the_kit` wants at 95%). Minutes lost: 5. Proposed skill text (project.md, Variants, after the monolithic-generator sentence): "When the original's own pipeline differs from the family's (margins, per-LOD UV scale, authored masks), match it through the family pipeline's call-time globals: `_house_longhouse.build_piece` reads `build`, `UV_MARGIN`, `BAKE_MARGIN`, `uv` and `fill_unbaked` from its module at call time, so a variant can swap in the original's unwrap and a masks pass without editing it (`_bakery_b.py`)."
2. **The parking sweep cannot see a variant.** `hex-sim/tests/parking.rs` `BAKERY` is a constant list of boxes taken from bakery_shop's LOD0, so the sweep passes whatever the variant's shape. Fix: `_bakery_b.ENVELOPE` holds those boxes in the piece's frame (tile (x, z) = (-4.1 + y, -8.5 + x)) and `envelope_errors` fails the build when any vertex below 2.7 m (car height plus margin; bakery_shop's own jetty and eaves sit above it unboxed, its door canopy below it boxed) falls outside them; the variant's shop front and pentice got one added box, which the patch also adds to `BAKERY`, so the sweep now covers both pieces. Minutes lost: 4. Proposed skill text (project.md, Variants): "A variant in a slot with parking must stay inside the hex-sim obstacle boxes of its slot below car height, or add its own box to that table: the table is constant and never reads the GLB. Assert it per LOD in the generator."
3. **bakery_shop has no `FOOTPRINTS` row.** It takes the default 1.0 m ground reach, so a `FOOTPRINTS` row for the variant alone would fail `hero_variants_fit_inside_the_authored_piece` (reach above 1.0). Fix: none for either piece, mirroring the original; its `GROUND_BOXES` row is what grass and walk use. Pre-existing gap, not changed here: bakery_shop's scatter clearance and walk fallback hull use that 1.0 m default; the bakery tile has no scatter rules, so nothing places inside it today. Minutes lost: 2. Skill: no change (the barn's proposed text on reading the authored box and reach first covers it).
4. **A kicked hip at a steeper pitch overshoots the authored LOD0 bound.** bakery_shop's LOD0 x minimum is -2.831 (its 0.04 m course kick at 50°); the variant's hip at 52° with the same kick would sit at -2.8315, and hip caps starting at the eave corners would stick out diagonally by 6 cm. Fix: kick 0.035 m, hip caps start 0.15 m in from the corners. Found by computing the bounds before the first build, not by a failed assert. Minutes lost: 1. Skill: no change.
5. **One refused shell call** (a no-op heredoc of my own). Minutes lost: 1. Skill: no change (SKILL.md "Worktree sessions" already forbids heredocs).
6. **The course kick put LOD0's back-left eave corner 0.083 m from the hex border** (owner A2, 2026-10-20: pull it in to 0.12 m, as bakery_shop's 0.119). LOD1, LOD2 and the collider sat at 0.120, at the plain eave corner (-2.8, -3.8); LOD0's kicked eave ring pushed that hip corner out by KICK·sin(pitch) = 2.76 cm on both axes, 3.7 cm along the border normal. The build checked the bakery boxes and the parking envelope, never the border. Fix: on LOD0's eave ring only that corner slides 2.76 cm up its hip line (`eave_ring`), so it stays on both kicked planes and lands on (-2.8, -3.8) in plan, 3.5 cm higher than the other eave corners; `border_clearance` now asserts at least 0.12 m per LOD in the slot. The collider, LOD1 and LOD2 positions are unchanged; the masks atlas repacked (walls 23.34 px/m, 0 unbaked triangles). Minutes lost: 0 (found at owner review). Proposed skill text (pitfalls.md, Coordinate frame and export): "A course kick moves a hip or eave corner out by kick·sin(pitch) on both axes, past the plain eave's bounds; a building near the hex border must assert its distance to the border per LOD in its slot, not only its bounds box (`_bakery_b.border_clearance`)."
7. **Native play shots follow the real mouse pointer.** The corner before/after runs differed by an extra line along the tile border in one run and a whole meadow ghost in another: the hovered-slot outline and the ghost track the pointer wherever it rests over the window, so two runs of one script disagree. One retry was also all black (mean 0) at host load 350 and the next run was fine. Minutes lost: 4. Proposed skill text (pitfalls.md, Look-dev and engine shots): "Native play-scenario shots pick up the real pointer: the hovered-slot outline and the selected tile's ghost follow it, so before/after runs differ.
