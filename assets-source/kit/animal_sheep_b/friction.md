# animal_sheep_b friction (variant of animal_sheep; see also animal_cow_b/friction.md)

1. **A uniform 0.6 scale reads as a small sheep, not a lamb.** A lamb's head and legs are large for its body. Fix: per-part maps in the generator: body, neck and tail at 0.58, head and ears at 0.72 with the muzzle at 0.88 of that (SNOUT), legs 0.66 high and 0.60 apart with thinner sections (LEG_W 0.8). Every part keeps the sheep's stations, so `check_buried` and the motion contract hold unchanged. Minutes lost: about 4 (one rebuild after the first still). No skill change needed.
2. **The fleece relief is in metres.** Painting at rest positions on the sheep returns the sheep's 6 mm fleece lumps, which on a 0.58x body read as coarse. Fix: `RestPaint(height_scale=BODY_S)`. Minutes lost: 0 (designed in). No skill change needed.
3. **Texel density is far above the floor (median 928 px/m).** The lamb's islands pack into the same 1024 atlas at 791 px/m; a 512 albedo would hold about 460 px/m and save about 0.4 MB of KTX2. Kept at 1024 to stay on the approved family's map sizes (`_animals.ATLAS`, asserted by `_animals_var`); the family is at 0.59 MB BC7, well inside the 1.5 MB piece budget. Owner question in the report.
