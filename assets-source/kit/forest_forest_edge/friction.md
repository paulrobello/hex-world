# forest_forest_edge family friction (forest_forest_edge_e0 .. _e5)

Status: six per-edge variants built, compressed, provenanced and gated; **pending owner piece approval** (each `assets-source/kit/forest_forest_edge_e<k>/approvals.json` holds one `status: "pending"` entry with no owner name; the gate reads only `gate: "reference"` approvals, so it grants nothing). References: the approved forest refs (`assets-source/refs/forest`). Builder: `scripts/blender/kit/_forest_edge.py`; thin generators `forest_forest_edge_e<k>.py`.

1. **forest_base is not 60-degree symmetric, but one edge piece is rotated to every edge** (about 15 min of design, then a rebuild). Its rim contours (`band_rim`, `band_floor`: sin 5/7/11/23 phi) and relief (`undulation`, `mounds` in local x, y) differ per wedge, and a piece at local edge k lies over the base's wedge k. The first build (one piece, commit 4117099) lifted every vertex over the highest of the six rotated caps, which floated up to 0.18 m (mean 0.06 m) over the litter at the inner join: a visible hump. Fix (coordinator decision D3): six variants, one per local edge; each is forest_base's own LOD cap for its wedge, rotated onto edge 0, clipped with `bisect_plane` to the slice and lifted 2 cm, so it follows the cap exactly (float over the floor now 0.02 m, the lift, in every variant and LOD). The forest_base formulas are copied into the builder and checked against forest_base's committed LOD0 `receiverSignature` on every build. Skill: `pitfalls.md` (Biome bases).
2. **Clipping a slice off a cap breaks the LOD ratios** (5 min). The clipped LOD2 keeps forest_base's 17 border-ring segments and contours, about 27% of the clipped LOD0. Splitting LOD0's longest interior edges (never the border ring) is exact on flat triangles and lifts LOD0 to about 950 tris, LOD2 at 10-14%. A first pass that split every edge quadrupled LOD0 and the GLB (59 KB) and also split the border ring, which broke the LOD-invariant section; the length-first split keeps the GLBs at about 40 KB.
3. **Corner rays** (owner choice pending, `stills/corner-options/`). O-a (shipped): each variant runs 0.6 m past its rays while diving under the cap, so two forest edges of one tile meet exactly on the shared ray and a non-forest corner shows a straight slope. O-b (mock only, `ob_mock_build.py`): the cover shrinks over the last 22 degrees toward a tapered ray into forest_base's own rim and mossy band. The mock classifies refined faces by centroid, so its outline is sawtoothed; a shipped O-b needs bisect cuts along the curve and an exact border end point.
