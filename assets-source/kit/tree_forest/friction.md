# tree_forest friction log

Snags hit while building the forest spruce (scripted route, headless only, no live Blender). Each entry: what happened, the fix, minutes lost, and the skill change.

1. **Thin needles alone hold no alpha coverage.** The first frond tiles (a twig, alternating side shoots, 1.25 px needles) measured 0.27 at mip 0 and 0 at the last mips. Fix: a shadowed frond body (dark needle green, alpha 1, 0.74 of the frond envelope) under the needles, plus denser shoots and longer needles: 0.561 at mip 0, 0.56-0.63 through mip 8, 1.0 at the last. Lost: about 5 min. Skill: `pitfalls.md` (foliage).
2. **The first crown read as thin plates around a lit cone.** Flat 0.4-wide cards on 21 whorls showed as horizontal plates from the side, and the inner core (0.5 of the crown radius, mid green) showed between them. Fix: 26 whorls, cards 0.55 of their length wide and folded 34 degrees (sides hang like ref-2's tiers), LOD0 cards at 2 segments instead of 3 to pay for it, and the core at 0.36 of the crown radius in near-black green. Lost: about 10 min. Skill: `pitfalls.md` (foliage, conifer core).
3. **Drooping branch tips crossed the gate's still band.** Lowest-whorl droop plus the fold drop put card corners at z -0.2 m. Fix: lowest whorl at 1.95 m, an upturned tip (`CURL`), and the generator asserts the lowest card vertex stays above 0.6 m. Lost: about 3 min. Skill: none (covered by the existing wind-band rule in `project.md`).
4. **One-segment cards would fail the fringe wind median.** With only a root and a tip row per card, half the fringe vertices sit at the trunk's sway and half at 1, so the median lands on the boundary. Fix: LOD1 and LOD2 main cards keep 2 segments (flat), so the middle row (U about 0.57) is the median. Lost: 0 (caught in planning). Skill: `pitfalls.md` (foliage).
5. **A lone trunk bakes AO of about 1.** `bake_to` correctly hides the cards, core and ground, so the relief AO of the trunk is 0.94-1.0. Fix: the masks AO multiplies an analytic height term over the bark island (ground contact 0.72 at the base, crown shade 0.85 above the lowest whorl); bark AO mean 0.85, p05 0.72. A first pass at 0.55 / 0.7 rendered the shaded lower trunk near black in engine. Lost: about 3 min. Skill: none (piece-specific).
6. **The six-view render is untextured.** `make kit-renders` renders the GLB, which embeds no images, so alpha cards render as solid quads and the needles cannot be judged there. Fix: self-review from a scratch headless EEVEE still script that binds the staged maps (alpha clip on the needles, the bark set at the slot's tileUvScale and tint). Lost: about 5 min. Skill: `pitfalls.md` (look-dev).
7. **Budget class.** The four 1024² maps compress to 1.31 MB (BC7) and 1.30 MB (ASTC), inside the `piece` class (1.5 MB), so the scatter tree stays in `piece` rather than `hero`. Lost: 0. Skill: none.
8. **Pale card tops and a black trunk in the first in-engine shot.** Needle roughness 0.55-0.7 with a strong needle normal tilt gave a grey sky sheen on up-facing cards, and the trunk and core under the dense crown (sun-shadowed, AO 0.55, dark tint) read as holes. Fix: needle roughness 0.72-0.88, needle normal tilt 0.35, the core colour raised to (0.03, 0.055, 0.024), the bark tint to (0.92, 0.8, 0.72) and the analytic AO lifted (entry 5). Lost: about 8 min. Skill: none (look tuning).
