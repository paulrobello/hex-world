# house_cottage friction log

1. **Roof normals pointed inward.** The roof is a counter-clockwise (x, z) profile swept along +y, which is a left-handed frame, so the obvious quad winding gave inward normals: the course tops baked AO 0 and would be back-face culled in Bevy. A `recalc_face_normals` pass did not save it. Fix: wind the swept quads `(ring0[i], ring1[i], ring1[j], ring0[j])`; checked by counting up- and down-facing roof triangles per slope on the reimported GLB. Minutes lost: 10. Skill: no change. This is ordinary mesh-building care, and the builder comment states the frame.
2. **A ridge point listed on both slopes collapsed the gable end caps.** Each slope's profile ended on the ridge point, the weld (`remove_doubles`) merged the two copies, and the verge caps rendered as stripes. Fix: the ridge point is listed once (`roof_profile`). Minutes lost: 10. Skill: no change. This one is specific to the builder.
3. **The plaster set's first bake used feature scales picked for 1 / scale.** The torus coordinates run 12.6 units per 2 m tile, so grit at Noise 220 was below a pixel. Fix: trowel 4.5, grit 60, pits Voronoi 10, judged on the 2×2 tile. Minutes lost: 5. Skill: `pitfalls.md` "Tileable sets" (the sliced-Voronoi entry covers cell size).
4. **A shared-helper edit invalidated the gated cottage.** Adding window `bars` and the quoin step to `_house.py` for the two-storey changed the cottage's `generatorDeps` hash. Fix: rebuild, recompress and re-provenance the cottage. The GLB came out byte-identical (471a6990…). Minutes lost: 5. Skill: no change. `stages.md` already says helpers are frozen once a piece is approved. Before the first piece's gate, finish the shared helper for the whole family.
5. **The nearest-face UV transfer broke LOD1 and LOD2.** LOD1/LOD2 took UV0 from LOD0 through a `DATA_TRANSFER` modifier (`POLYINTERP_NEAREST`). Faces whose corners sat on edges shared by two LOD0 islands took corners from different islands. Those faces stretched across the atlas (LOD1 max density 3,966 px/m against a median of 57), and 11 of 12 LOD1 glass faces lost their night glow. Nothing in the gate or the LOD0-only 6-view render showed it. A per-LOD check on the reimported GLB found it (density spread, and masks.B sampled at each glass face's UV centroid). Fix: one 1024² atlas across all three LODs, each LOD baked onto its own islands (first bake clears, 4 px margin inside 12 px island spacing). Every LOD now measures 15-18 px/m with no outliers, and every glass face reads B = 1.0. Minutes lost: 20. Skill: `pitfalls.md`, "Leaves, foliage and LODs".
6. **Masks fill (card 01a15544869e, 2026-10-19).** 74 of 1,048 LOD0 and 22 of 436 LOD1 triangles (4.36 m² and 0.97 m²: glazing bars, frame returns, timber sides, tile-course butts) sat on masks texels the bake never wrote, so they sampled A = 0 and R = 0 (black, no snow); the hooks gate checks a slot's maximum and missed it. Fix: `_house.fill_unbaked` (from barn_red/bakery_shop) after the bake, plus `_house.centroid_samples` asserted at 0 per LOD in the build. The committed check is `docs/checkpoints/houses-masks-fill/masks_centroids.mjs` on the compressed GLB (it reproduces 74/22/0 on the old files). The GLB stays byte-identical; the fill writes unwritten texels only. Minutes lost: 0. Skill: proposed `pitfalls.md` text replacing the last sentence of "At house-scale masks densities": "`_house.build_piece` runs the fill and asserts 0 unbaked triangles per LOD (house_cottage had 74 LOD0 / 22 LOD1, house_twostorey 86 / 36, before 2026-10-19); `docs/checkpoints/houses-masks-fill/masks_centroids.mjs` checks a compressed GLB."
7. **A tighter island gap raised density but darkened the mips.** The bakery's pack (`pack_islands` FRACTION 0.006, 2 px bake margin) took the cottage from 18.15 to 22.24 px/m and the two-storey from 15.21 to 19.03 on every LOD, with the same look at mip 0. Box-filtered and sampled area-weighted per slot (`geometry_and_mips.mjs`), mips 2-3 lost up to 0.08 A on frames, timbers, glass and stone: at a 6 px gap the unwritten background reaches an island one mip earlier, which reads darker from play distance. Reverted to `atlas_unwrap(margin=0.012)` with a 4 px bake margin (density unchanged, GLB byte-identical). Also, margin methods do not compare: 0.012 FRACTION gave 12.0 px/m against the default (scaled) method's 18.15. Minutes lost: 25 (one full rebuild cycle). Skill: proposed `pitfalls.md` addition to the bakery's island-margin line: "A smaller island gap buys density at mip 0 and costs it at mips 2-3, where the unwritten background bleeds in sooner (houses, 2026-10-19: 6 px raised density 22% and darkened thin members by up to 0.08 A at mip 3). Judge a margin change on box-filtered mips, area-weighted per slot, and compare margins under one `pack_islands` margin method."
8. **EEVEE six-view renders are not byte-stable under heavy load.** `make kit-renders` on a byte-identical bakery GLB changed `front.jpg` on 436 px by up to 16 levels (host load about 200), and the same happened once on house_twostorey. `gate.render_six_views` already strips date metadata, so this is EEVEE sampling, not metadata. Fix: restored the committed render of the unchanged GLB. Minutes lost: 5. Skill: proposed `gates.md` Determinism row note: "six-view renders are byte-identical for an unchanged GLB at normal load; under heavy host load EEVEE can differ by a few levels on a few hundred px: compare GLB SHAs, not render bytes, before calling a geometry change."
