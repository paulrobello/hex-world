# bakery_shop friction log

1. **Island margins, not area, set a building's masks density.** The first 1024² unwrap at the family's `island_margin` 0.012 (about 12 px) measured 8.7 px/m on LOD0 walls: a timber-framed front with glazing bars, tile-course butts and beam sides is thousands of small islands, and every one pays the margin. Cutting the hidden faces (roof undersides inside the walls, the plinth top under the floor, the walls behind the plinth, the bay and the wing) helped little; margin 0.006 (6 px) with a 2 px bake margin took LOD0 to 20.7 px/m, LOD1 10.3 and the bow-window panes 46.5 (2.5x boost). Minutes lost: 10. Proposed skill text (`pitfalls.md`, beside "At house-scale masks densities"): "On a building with many thin members the island margin, not the surface area, sets the masks density: at 1024², `island_margin` 0.012 left the bakery's walls at 8.7 px/m and 0.006 with a 2 px bake margin gave 20.7. Keep the island gap at least 2 x bake margin + 2 px, and scale LOD1/LOD2 islands down before packing (`bakery_shop.py` `LOD_UV_SCALE`)."
2. **A wall plate inside the roof slab.** The `_house` wall-plate convention (`EAVE - 0.08`) put the bakery's plate and post tops inside the 50° roof's slab, whose underside meets the wall line at 4.18 m (0.14 m slab / cos 50°), so they baked A = 0 and spent texels unseen. Found by listing every LOD triangle whose masks centroid reads A < 0.1 on the staged GLB. Fix: plate at `EAVE - 0.32`, upper windows lowered to clear it. Minutes lost: 5. Skill: no change (the "unique masks bake spends texels on every face" pitfall covers it); the dark-centroid listing is the check worth keeping (below).
3. **The brief's 512² masks did not hold the family's density.** The brief named a 512² masks; at 512² the bakery's walls would be about 10 px/m against the houses' 15-18 and the barn's 1024². Built at 1024² (`project.md` was updated to 1024² by the barn run the same day). Minutes lost: 0. Skill: none (already folded).
4. **Bread in a window needs colour, not AO.** `masks.A` scales only ambient light in `kit_material.wesl` (diffuse_occlusion), so loaf shapes authored into A vanish in direct sun. Each shelf row of the bay is split into a warm loaf band (`loaf_glass`) and a dark interior band (`display_glass`), and the A/B loaf pattern rides on top for shade and night. Minutes lost: 0. Proposed skill text (`project.md`, Buildings): "Detail inside glass (shop goods) is colour bands on their own slots plus an authored A/B pattern: masks.A only scales ambient light, so A-only detail disappears on the sun side (`bakery_shop.py` `loaf_field`)."
6. **Yawed pieces shade their walls as if unrotated (renderer, not asset).** Every in-engine shot read the bakery's walls near-black, the sun-facing front darker than the back. Normals on the exported GLB agree with the winding on every slot (0 of 1,405 LOD0 triangles disagree), the bake is clean, and shadows off changes nothing. The decisive frame (`docs/checkpoints/bakery_shop/yaw-check.json`, `--shadows off`): the bakery's +x gable (N.L 0.55 at yaw 90) reads unlit, and in one frame of house_cluster the yaw-0 house_twostorey's +x front is lit while both house_cottages (yaw 90 and 270) are dark on their camera-facing +x walls under lit roofs. Pieces placed with a hero `yaw_deg` look shaded with normals that miss the yaw. The barn (yaw 30) "near-black shade side" owner question may share the cause. Not fixed here (crates are orchestrator-owned); the sheet's in-engine cells are labelled not representative. Minutes lost: 20. Proposed skill text (`pitfalls.md`, Look-dev and engine shots): "Before judging a dark wall in engine, shoot one `--shadows off` frame with a yaw-0 piece's lit face beside the yawed one: a monotonic grade cannot make a face turned from the sun brighter than one facing it, so if it does, the renderer is mis-rotating normals (bakery_shop, 2026-10-19)."
