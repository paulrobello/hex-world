# barn_red_b friction log

1. **The brief assumed a barn helper to import; there is none.** `barn_red.py` is a monolithic generator: it calls `main()` at import and keeps its geometry (wall slabs, trim strips, frames, battens, braced doors, the roof-course profile) in its own module, so a variant cannot import it, and a non-`_` import would not be hashed into `generatorDeps` anyway (a provenance hole). Extracting a `_barn.py` out of it edits an approved generator and stales the barn. Fix: `_barn_b.py` drives `_house_longhouse.build_piece` through its module globals (`build`, `COTTAGE_BOUNDS`, `COTTAGE_LOD0_BOUNDS`), as `_house_slate.py` does, so the masks bake, `fill_unbaked`, the zero-unbaked-triangle assert, `budgets.check`, the LOD2 box collider and the export are the approved house family's (barn_red uses the same 1024² masks, 64 samples, 0.6 m AO, 0.012 UV margin and 4 px bake margin), and re-derives only barn_red's monolithic geometry steps for the asymmetric section (project.md, Variants). Minutes lost: 5. Proposed skill text (SKILL.md "Helpers", after the module-globals sentence): "When the original is monolithic (it runs on import, as `barn_red.py` does), drive a family pipeline that matches its bake settings through module globals (`_barn_b.py` over `_house_longhouse.build_piece`) and copy only the geometry steps; never import the generator or extract a helper from it, which stales the approved piece."
2. **A variant's footprint is pinned by the authored piece's, not by the tile.** `scatter::hero_variants_fit_inside_the_authored_piece` requires the variant's `GROUND_BOXES` entry to nest inside barn_red's (no tolerance) and its footprint reach not to exceed it, and `ground_boxes_match_the_kit` wants the box within 1 cm of the collider. So the brief's "longer barn" option was out from the start: barn_red's collider is the whole 10.6 x 7.7 m roof. Fix: the variant changes the section instead (a catslide over an open lean-to on the yard side, the ridge 1.75 m lower, a cupola), with its LOD2 shell exactly barn_red's box and every LOD inside barn_red's LOD0 extents measured on its GLB (x ±5.34, y ±3.878), asserted by `_house_longhouse.footprint_errors`. Minutes lost: 3. Proposed skill text (project.md, Variants): "A hero variant's collider must nest inside the authored piece's ground box and reach (`hero_variants_fit_inside_the_authored_piece`); read those from the authored GLB before designing the variant's plan, and vary the section or the roof when the box is already full."
