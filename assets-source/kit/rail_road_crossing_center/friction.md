# rail_road_crossing_center friction (2026-10-19)

- **"Additive" helper edits still stale approved pieces.** The brief allowed additive changes to `_rail.py`, but the gate re-hashes each helper file whole, so even a new function in `_rail.py` would mark rail_straight_center and rail_bend_60_center stale and schedule their rebuild and re-review. Fix: all crossing code lives in a new `kit/_rail_crossing.py` that imports `_rail`, `_road` and `_road_bend` unchanged; `make kit-check` prints no stale warning. 0 minutes lost (caught before writing). Proposed SKILL.md text under "Helpers": "Put a new piece's code in a new `_<piece-family>.py` that imports the approved helpers; adding to an approved helper, even a new function, re-hashes it and stales every piece that imports it."
- **Mixed lane kinds on one centre needed no gate change.** `lane_joint_errors` resolves each lane's kind from that lane's own socket `extras.kind`, so road lanes 1 and 4 are compared with `road_straight_edge` at x = 6 and rail lanes 0 and 3 find no rail edge piece. A rail lane is therefore checked only by the generator (`_rail.border_section_errors`). Fix: an additive pin test, `kit_gate::mixed_kind_centre_checks_each_lane_against_its_own_kind`, asserts the per-lane kinds and non-empty road joint sections in every LOD so the road check cannot pass vacuously. 5 minutes. Proposed gates.md text (Seam: lanes): "A centre may mix network kinds (the level crossing: rail 0/3, road 1/4); each lane is checked against the edge pieces of its own socket's kind."
- **A fixed deck pushes LOD2 over 15%.** The full deck (three plank bands, flangeway walls and floors, 7 lateral strips) is about 150 tris in every LOD, which alone is 9% of a 1,640-tri LOD0. Fix: LOD2 (past 400 m) draws one slab 2 mm above the rail heads with its two end faces; LOD2 = 186 tris (11.3%). 0 minutes (sized before the first build). No skill change: `gates.md` already says to size LODs from the floor formula.
- **Verge meeting the ballast top.** The road verge ramps up to meet the ballast shoulder at the deck edge; ending it exactly at the ballast top would make two coplanar slots (grass and gravel) z-fight. Fix: the verge ends 3 cm above the ballast top with a turf-edge face down into it (`VERGE_END`, `EDGE_FOOT`). 0 minutes. No skill change.
- **The north-facing gate faces read near-black in engine.** The shade-side close-up and the eye-level shot showed white gates as dark navy. Per pitfall 143 it was A/B'd against an approved light-walled piece before touching the asset: the gate hinge post's north face measures mean luma 32 (sRGB, 20 x 150 px patch), `house_twostorey`'s white plaster north wall 22, with shadows on and with `--shadows off` alike, and the same gates' south faces read white. So the masks are fine and faces turned from the kit sun get only the black-backdrop IBL; no asset change. The sheet labels carry the numbers. 8 minutes. Proposed pitfalls.md addition under "Look-dev and engine shots": "A white surface facing away from the kit sun (north and west faces) reads dark slate in engine at about luma 20-35, the same as house_twostorey's north wall; quote that comparison on the sheet so the owner does not read it as an asset fault."
- **The first two rail-seam camera framings missed the seam.** Aiming at the border midpoint from the south-west showed only continuous straight track, with nothing to show where the crossing ends. A camera due south of a point 3 m inside the border (deck and gates at the left, the straight running on to the right) reads as a seam. 6 minutes. No skill change.
- **gates.md names the wrong file for the gate.** Its line 4 says `kit_gate::*` tests live in `crates/hex-render/tests/kit_gate.rs`; they are in `crates/hex-play/tests/kit_gate.rs` (`make kit-check` runs `cargo test -p hex-play --test kit_gate`). 1 minute. Proposed fix: change that path in `references/gates.md` line 4.
- **Ramp grade on the acute side.** The 60-degree skew makes the deck edge 2.4 m from the r = 6 joint on one kerb line and 5.3 m on the other; the smoothstep ramp peaks at 21% on the short side (9% on the long side, 12% on the crown). Not tuned: reported to the owner with the eye-level shot. `cars.rs` `ROAD_SURFACE_Y` is a constant 0.11 m, so cars on a future crossing tile would drive through the 0.46 m deck (owner question).
