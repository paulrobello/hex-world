# animal_cow_b friction (variant of animal_cow, shared with animal_sheep_b through `kit/_animals_var.py`)

1. **A posed variant cannot reuse a monolithic generator, and the paint is position-driven.** `animal_cow.py` paints from each texel's creature-frame position (patches are ellipsoids in space), so a turned head painted where it now stands would pick up the wrong patches. Fix: `_animals_var.RestPaint` maps each sample back to its rest position on the original (the head and ears by the inverse rigid turn, the re-lofted neck by `loft_map`, which keeps the same lateral and normal fractions at the same arc fraction), then calls the original's paint, copied unchanged. The blaze, eyes, muzzle and patches come out identical to animal_cow's. Minutes lost: about 5, at design time. Proposed skill text below (P1).
2. **The neck root kept a graze weight inside the body.** `_animals.Motion` takes NECK_BASE as a point; for a bent neck, the original's hand-picked base no longer sits where the neck leaves the chest. Fix: `_animals_var.neck_base` derives it as the largest projection on the pivot-to-muzzle axis of any neck surface point inside the body. Minutes lost: 2. No skill change: it is in the helper.
3. **Farm.ron spot: the meadow's only flat ground is taken.** The paddock's relief floats a hoof by at most 2 cm only along one diagonal valley from (-1, 10) to (1.5, 6.5), where animal_cow and the near sheep already stand. A grazing cow's footprint disc (1.4 m) cannot fit there beside animal_cow's (1.5 m); the best disc-clear spot floats 6 cm, the best body-clear spot (oriented boxes, 0.3 m gap) 3.9 cm. Moving animal_cow too gets both to 2.2 cm. Handed to the orchestrator as options (report). Minutes lost: about 10 (spot searches in `<scratchpad>/animalvar-spots/`). Proposed skill text below (P2).
4. **LOD2 still shows AO-black texels at the neck and leg-top junctions (open, not rebuilt).** The four-station LOD2 head fixed animal_cow's beak (friction note 6 there), but the coarse LOD2 body sits inside LOD0's surface, so texels LOD0 buries (AO 0) show at the junctions in `docs/checkpoints/animal_variants/animal_*_b-eevee-lod2.jpg`. Same open item as animal_cow note 6; LOD2 draws only beyond 400 m. Fix at the next family rebuild: fill hidden texels' AO from their visible neighbours. Visible AO p05 is 0.655 here against animal_cow's 0.749, because the lowered head and neck sit under the chest. Minutes lost: 2 (found in the end stills). **Closed 2026-10-20 by lod2-neck-fix** (`_animals.fill_hidden_ao`; numbers in animal_cow friction note 7).
5. **The `_animals` extras describe a nose-down graze; this cow is already down.** `grazeDegToGround` is 0 and the shared `graze` text still reads "nose down". A P3 head raise on animal_cow_b pitches V > 0 nose up about `neckPivot` (the generator's docstring says so). Left for P3 rather than rebuilding. Minutes lost: 0.
6. **`sed -i` slipped into a docstring edit** against the brief's rule; caught on read-back and fixed with the Edit tool. Minutes lost: 1. No change: the rule is already in the brief.

## Proposed skill text (for the orchestrator to fold in)
- **P1, project.md (Variants):** "A posed or rescaled variant of a creature (`kit/_animals_var.py`) moves the original's station table through per-part maps and paints every sample at its rest position on the original (`RestPaint`): position-driven paint (patch ellipsoids, face masks along a loft) then lands on the same anatomy. Re-loft a bent neck along a curve (`curve_part`) and map it back with `loft_map`; derive the graze-weight base from the geometry (`neck_base`)."
- **P2, pitfalls.md (Look-dev and engine shots, or a farm note):** "Before proposing a hero spot on a meadow tile, map the relief float (-relief(x, -z)) under every hoof and contact at the candidate yaw, not just the pivot: on the farm the flat ground (float <= 2 cm) is one diagonal valley, already holding animal_cow and a sheep."
