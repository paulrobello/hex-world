### F2. Socket compatibility (`data/sockets.ron`, symmetric)

Legal pairs: G–G, G–F, F–F, R–R, V–V, B–B, B–G, B–O, O–O. Everything else is illegal, in particular R–G (no dead ends), V–G, O–G and R–V.

### F3. Lane contract

- Road: one lane per road edge, `offset = 0.0`, `width = 6.0` m (`scripts/blender/kit/_road.py` `ROAD_WIDTH`, `hexgeo.lane_frame`).
- River: one lane per river edge, `offset = 0.0`, `width = 8.0` m water width. Provisional: the river asset family may change the width, and then updates `data/sockets.ron` and every river tile in the same commit.
- Lanes must match offset and width across a seam (§5.2). Height is 0 and the profile is `Flat` for every MVP edge (D5).

### F4. Pending pieces

The §5.3 validator requires every referenced kit piece and every rotation thumbnail to exist. Most MVP pieces do not exist yet, so the validator reads an explicit pending list, `data/pending-pieces.ron`, and fails on any reference that is neither in `assets/kit/manifest.json` nor pending. Thumbnails are pending as a whole until the thumbnail renderer exists. Removing an entry from the pending list is how a finished piece becomes enforced. The list never grows silently: every entry names the stream or card that will deliver it.
