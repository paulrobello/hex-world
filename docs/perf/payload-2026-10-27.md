# Shipped payload and music bitrates, 2026-10-27

Card "Revisit music bitrates against the full payload". The P2 content is in. This measures the full shipped payload once, then tests trial music bitrates. Nothing shipped has changed: a cut needs the owner's sign-off by ear (`docs/review/music-bitrate.html`).

## Method

- `make build-web` at `4897f2e`, measured on the `dist/` it wrote.
- Bytes are counted as GitHub Pages serves them, by the rule in `scripts/size/initial-download.mjs`: `.wasm`, `.js`, `.html`, `.json` and `.glb` gzipped (zlib level 9), everything else raw. Audio and KTX2 are therefore raw.
- The initial download and the per-pack kit totals come from that script's own `account()`, so they match `make initial-download`.
- MiB = 1,048,576 bytes.
- The wasm reads 9,000,542 B (8.58 MiB) under zlib level 9, the rule above. `make web-size` measures it with macOS `gzip -9`, which reads 8,879,443 B (8.47 MiB) for the same file. Both are the same build.

These measures are the same for every trial, so they rule out nothing:
- Integrated loudness is within 0.2 LU of the master (−18 LUFS), and the true peak is −4.2 dBTP or lower (ceiling −1).
- Every file decodes to exactly the master's frame count, so the loop points hold.
- The stereo width (side-to-mid energy) moves by 0.17 dB or less.

How to read the measures:
- **Band energy.** Opus keeps the energy above 12 and 16 kHz at every rate, because it fills high bands it cannot code exactly with shaped noise. Its loss at lower rates shows instead as a larger log-spectral distance and residual: the texture up there changes while the level holds.
- **AAC low-pass.** ffmpeg's native AAC encoder low-passes near 16 kHz at 96k and near 12 kHz at 64k. meadow-day and hearth-town have real content there (−34 dB re the mix over 16 kHz), so they lose an audible octave of air.
- **Loop seam.** AAC 96k also fails the `audio-check` loop seam test on photo-mode: a wrap step of 0.0258 against the p99.9 step of 0.0198. `make audio-check` would refuse that encode.
- **Residual.** "Residual 4–12 kHz" is the energy of (decoded − master) in that band, re the master's. It is an objective proxy for added codec noise, not a perceptual score.
- **ViSQOL/PEAQ.** Neither was run.

## Recommendation

Keep the current rates (Opus 128k, AAC 160k).
- Music sits outside every gated budget. The initial download is 21.3 of 30 MiB, the wasm 8.58 of 12 MiB, and the deployed site 360 MiB of 600 MB.
- The most a full session would save is 2.75 MB at Opus 96k, streamed in the background after the first gesture.

If the owner wants the bytes back anyway, the floor is **Opus 96k and AAC 128k**.
- On the objective measures, Opus 96k changes only the noise-filled texture above about 12 kHz, and AAC 128k keeps the top octave.
- They save 23% and 20% of the music.
- Cut only after a listen on `docs/review/music-bitrate.html` finds no difference on meadow-day, the brightest cue.

Do not use Opus 64k or AAC 96k and below:
- Opus 64k shows the largest texture change.
- AAC 96k drops everything over 16 kHz and fails the photo-mode loop check.

The bitrate decision is pending the owner's listen.
