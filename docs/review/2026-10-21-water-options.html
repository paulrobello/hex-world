Water look options, 2026-10-21 (A3)

Owner A3 Q1 and Q2 asked to see options for the drying trail's strength and the river churn foam's level; Q3 asked that the fallback river bed draw no drying trail. Every image is a native capture at 1280x800 on the default grade rich-3 with fixed clocks (--clock fixed --water_clock fixed), tilt-shift off, seed 1, the same camera per view in every option, and the review-only flag --water_look trail=<x>,churn=<g> (docs/agent-operability.md). The shipped defaults are unchanged: without the flag the shaders get exactly today's values, and make thumbs reproduces every committed thumbnail byte for byte. Each option pairs one trail value with one churn value in a single run, so both questions share the clock. Reproduce with bash docs/review/water-options/shots.sh, then build.py and page.py in the same folder.

Q1: drying trail strength

When the wash retreats, the sand it just left stays soaked and dries back to the damp band. The option value is the colour factor of that soaked sand (lower is darker); the dry-back time (about 1.1 s), the gloss and the damp band are the same in every option. The same factor also darkens the soaked line under the live run-up, so the edge of the wash changes with it. Each strip is a crop of the straight beach's waterline at five moments of one retreat (click the first frame for the whole view). The difference is a thin band right above the water, so the side-by-side clip shows it most clearly.

Q2: churn foam level

Churn foam is the white water where the river's flow turns or slows: the fork's meeting water and the bends, none on straights, fading out at every tile border. The option value scales it (today 1.0). Ripple flecks and the other foam (rapids, river mouth, coast) are the same in every option. It scales with how sharply the flow turns, so the tight bend and the fork show it and the gentle river_bend shows almost none at any level.

Q3: no drying trail on the fallback river bed

Confirmed, already the current behaviour; nothing changed. The fallback (placeholder) bed is drawn only while a water tile's kit base is missing, by assets/shaders/water_terrain.wesl. That shader has no drying trail at all: it has no trail flag and never looks at past wash positions. On a river bed it also zeroes the live run-up (reach = wash_amp * swash * (1 - river)), so a fallback river bed shows only the static damp gradient at the waterline. The kit river beds (straights, bends, fork, bridges, spring) carry no wet band either.

One related case for the owner: the river mouth bases (river_mouth_base, river_mouth_l_base) are kit beach pieces with the wet band, so the mouth's sandy fan does draw the drying trail where it meets the sea, the same as a beach. Nothing was changed there.
