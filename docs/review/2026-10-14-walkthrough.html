Q7 Web frame-latency limit on by default (F2: stall tail)

With frames uncapped, Chrome's WebGPU backlog makes the compositor swap block for 48–67 ms about every 50 frames. The opt-in ?frame_latency=3 removes it for about 0.4 ms mean. At a normal 60 Hz display there are no stalls either way.

Recommendation: Turn it on by default. It is harmless at 60 Hz and removes the tail on fast GPUs with high-refresh displays. That closes the PRD stall-tail box.
