A37Where the title theme plays

Q1
title-theme (179 s, approved with the music set) is mastered, but the game has no title screen and no event plays it, so it never sounds. As of today it is left out of the deployed site (it was 7 MB of the deploy that nothing fetched) until something plays it. Browsers only allow sound after the first click, so it cannot play over the opening load.

Play it when a new, empty world starts (after the New world click), fading into the biome music once the first tiles go down. Recommended: the closest moment to a title screen the game has now.
O2
Play it on the world list (Worlds sheet) while it is open.
O3
Keep it unshipped until a title or menu screen exists.

A38P4: how a player sets a tile's height

Q1
P4 adds height levels 0-3 (4 m a level). This answer shapes the data model, the save migration and whether the per-rotation thumbnails stay valid, so it comes first.
O1
Auto, like auto-rotate: a tile takes the level its neighbours imply, and R/scroll cycles the legal levels when more than one fits. Tiles store heights relative to their own level, so thumbnails stay as they are. Recommended.
O2
A raise/lower tool: placed tiles are lifted or lowered with keys or a bar tool; placement checks the edges.
O3
Height is part of the tile type: plateau, slope and cliff tiles in the inventory, no level choice.

A39P4: rail and road grade

Q1
A one-tile ramp climbs 4 m over 27.7 m, about 14%. That reads fine for a stylised road but is far too steep for a steam train (real lines stay under about 3%).
O1
Roads ramp in one tile; rail never changes level and crosses height on viaducts and in tunnels. Recommended.
O2
Rail ramps span several tiles, which needs half or quarter edge heights and breaks the whole-level rule.
O3
Accept a stylised 14% rail ramp.

A40P4: mountains

Q1
How big a mountain is.
O1
Single-tile hills and peaks that rise inside the hex and meet neighbours at their edge level. Recommended for P4; bigger massifs come with the 7-hex mega-tile card.
O2
Mountains built by stacking levels (level-3 tiles with cliff and slope edges).
O3
Wait for mega tiles and make mountains 7-hex pieces.

A41P4: rivers that flow downhill

Q1
River lanes gain a flow direction, placement refuses a river that would run uphill, and waterfalls form where a river meets a cliff edge. Today's rivers have no direction.
O1
Saves load with flow derived from the sea and height (flat rivers keep flowing as now); new placement enforces downhill. Recommended.
O2
Rivers stay directionless on level ground; direction only matters on slopes and at waterfalls.

A42P4: how mountains and cliffs are modelled

Q1
PRD §11 sends mountains, cliffs and the volcano through live Blender MCP sculpting, but your rule since 2026-10-13 is headless Blender only.
O1
Headless procedural generators (displacement, erosion-style noise, baked like every piece so far). Recommended; the PRD gets corrected.
O2
Lift the headless rule for these hero pieces and sculpt them live.
