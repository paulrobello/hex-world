A46Grass and sway frame cost: the last P2 box

Q1
Measured on MiniMac (base M4, 2,066-tile world on High, 2560x1440, loads 3-6). Grass adds +2.1 ms (Sparse), +4.2 ms (Normal) and +6.4 ms (Lush) to the web mean (31.3 ms with grass off; native +3.3, +6.1, +9.1 ms on 36.5). Foliage sway adds +3.7 ms at the close camera and +1.3 ms at the overview (web, GPU time; draws and main thread unchanged). The P2 target, p95 at or under 16.6 ms on a 2,000-tile world, cannot hold on this GPU: with grass off every frame is already over 25 ms, and the frame is GPU-bound (main thread 16.9 of 31.5 ms). The M4 Max was not measured.
O1
Accept the measured cost as the P2 result and check the box; restate the target as a cost budget (grass at Normal and sway each under about 5 ms on a base M4), and file a card to cut the sway cost, which at 3.7 ms is more than half of Normal grass. Recommended.
O2
Keep the 16.6 ms target and measure on the M4 Max (macdaddy) in a quiet window before deciding.
O3
Keep P2 open until the sway and grass costs are cut.
