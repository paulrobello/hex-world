A36Music bitrates against the full payload

Q1
With the P2 content in, one player downloads about 120 MiB in a full session (BC7 textures, WebM audio); music is 15 MiB of it (12%), all streamed in the background when a cue first plays, never in the 21 MiB initial download. Cutting Opus 128 to 96 kbps (and AAC 160 to 128) saves about 2.75 MB in a session that hears four cues; loudness, loop points and stereo width are unchanged, and the high band keeps its energy but its texture changes (measured, not heard). Lower rates (Opus 64, AAC 96) audibly risk the top end and AAC 96 breaks a loop seam, so they are off the table. Listen to meadow-day, the brightest cue: http://127.0.0.1:8095/.claude/worktrees/agent-af09c276bdfa7ba55/docs/review/music-bitrate.html
O1
Keep the current bitrates (Opus 128k, AAC 160k). Recommended: music is outside every budget.
O2
Cut to Opus 96k and AAC 128k, if you hear no difference on meadow-day.
