A52Mega tiles: a 7-hex piece (centre plus ring)

Q1
Height: how does the massif meet the world? The scoping (card 01a13bcc6f8d notes) found every seam, cliff and ground rule is built per cell.
Q2
Networks: may roads and rivers cross the massif or end at it?
Q3
Tunnels: may a road tunnel run under a mega tile?
Q4
Placement and picker: how is it offered and placed?
Q5
Replace and bulldoze: what happens when a mega tile is replaced or removed?
Q6
Rules: the HUD mirrors the engine's placement rules in TypeScript, the biggest drift risk the scoping found. Mirror the 7-cell rules too, or have the HUD ask the engine?
Q1 O1
Its 12 outer edges sit flat at the tile's own level, like a raised Meadow, and the peak rises inside the footprint (recommended: neighbours keep today's seam rules; only the interior is new ground).
Q1 O2
A height field across the 7 cells, with outer edges at several levels.
Q2 O1
No roads or rivers in the first round: every outer edge is plain ground (recommended: road and river stitching stay per cell).
Q2 O2
Roads may cross on a pass or end at the foot.
Q3 O1
Refused under a mega tile in the first round (recommended).
Q3 O2
Allowed, with the massif as cover.
Q4 O1
Its own inventory entry; the ghost shows the whole footprint; it needs all 7 cells empty and at least one placed neighbour outside it; R turns it about the centre (recommended).
Q4 O2
Built from smaller pieces that merge when the ring is complete.
Q5 O1
Removed and undone as one tile; replacing a mega tile with a single tile, or the reverse, is refused (recommended).
Q5 O2
Replace swaps the whole footprint for single tiles.
Q6 O1
The HUD asks the engine through the bridge for mega-tile legality instead of mirroring it (recommended: one source of truth for the new rules).
Q6 O2
Mirror the rules in rules.ts with parity tests, as for single tiles.
