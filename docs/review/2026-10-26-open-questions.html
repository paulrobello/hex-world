A25B1 boulders and outcrop: references

Q1
Approve the references. Recommended: ref-1 sets the four boulders and ref-2 the outcrop; ref-3 (meadow and forest edge) and ref-4 (coast) set placement and look only, since they read larger than the sizes in Q2.
Q2
Sizes, all above the existing rock_small (1.9 m): boulders 2.2, 2.6, 3.4 and 4.5 m long, and an 8 x 5 x 1.2 m whaleback outcrop. Recommended as listed.
Q3
Where and how many: boulders on meadow, forest and oak tiles (about 0.3 to 0.4 per tile), the outcrop on meadow and forest (0.15 to 0.2), small boulders most common; none on farm, wheat and windmill tiles. Recommended.
Q4
Coast: boulders stand at the grass-to-sand line, not on the wet beach (the waves ignore props, so water would run through them), and count inside the beach tiles' 0 to 6 cap. Recommended.
Q5
Distance: every boulder stays at 150 m; past 400 m only the two big boulders and the outcrop remain, which keeps the draw budget. Recommended.
Q6
Texture: a unique bake per piece at about 128 px/m (recommended), or one shared tileable granite with a masks map per piece (cheaper, less unique).
Q7
The walker cannot climb onto the outcrop. Recommended.

A26B3 lily pads and reed bed: references

Q8
Approve the references. Recommended: ref-1 sets the pads' colour and form (dark olive, matching the spring's own pads, which they reuse); ref-3 and ref-4 are layout only (their pads came out lighter).
Q9
Pieces: three lily clusters (3 pads; 7 with a white flower; 5 with a pink bud, 1.8 to 2.8 m across) and a dense reed bed about 4 x 2 m and 2.4 m tall, distinct from the sparse cattail clumps on the bank. Recommended.
Q10
Where: the spring (always lilies, a reed bed half the time) and river bends; not straights, forks or mouths. They get their own 0 to 2 per tile so the riverbank's 0 to 4 stones and reeds keep their new half and half share. Recommended.
Q11
Never on the ocean, beach or river-mouth fan, where waves move the surface; reed beds root in shallow water 0.4 to 0.8 m deep. Recommended.
Q12
Distance: pads drop out past 400 m (as the spring's do); the reed bed keeps a low silhouette. Recommended.
