A47Back-to-back tunnel portals (P4)

Q1
Two road tunnel portals placed face to face with no cover tile between them form a complete tunnel of zero length: cars enter one portal and leave the other at once. It is harmless and the spec does not set a minimum length. Keep it, or refuse a tunnel with no cover?
O1
Keep it as is (recommended: no visual or routing fault, and changing it touches hex-core tunnel.rs, the HUD rules mirror and the parity tests).
O2
Refuse it: a tunnel needs at least one cover tile, and the HUD hint says why.

A48When to re-encode the legacy KTX2 files

Q1
95 of the 160 committed kit items were compressed before the encode was pinned to one thread with ASLR off (2026-10-21 and 26). Their files are valid and reproducible with the old flags, but each changes bytes once on its next re-encode, which dirties thumbnails and needs a look check. kit-compress now warns when it happens (card 01a185e1f1a6).
O1
Re-encode all 95 together with the KTX-Software 5.x upgrade, as one byte change, one thumbs pass and one review (recommended: the upgrade re-encodes everything anyway).
O2
Re-encode all 95 now and again at the upgrade.
O3
Leave them; each re-encodes at its next real rebuild.
