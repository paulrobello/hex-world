A29Engine drops to 60 Hz while a HUD control has focus

Q1
Found while fixing the frame pacing judder: whenever a HUD control has keyboard focus (opening Settings focuses its tab button), the engine falls to a 60 Hz update (Bevy's default for an unfocused window). On a 120 Hz display, opening Settings halves the frame rate. O1: keep the engine at full rate when the canvas is unfocused on the web (about one line; the browser already throttles background tabs). O2: the HUD hands focus back to the canvas when a sheet closes (still 60 Hz while a sheet is open). O3: leave it. Recommended: O1.
