# Waterfall references for the owner, 2026-11-22

WF. Waterfall fall-face references (P4 rivers at levels, Task 16 of `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-22-rivers-at-levels.md`)

Rivers at levels drop over a granite face where a river meets river one, two or three levels lower. These are the R2 reference candidates for the three fall faces, `waterfall_l1` (4 m), `waterfall_l2` (8 m) and `waterfall_l3` (12 m), built by one generator scaled by drop. Nothing is modelled until you pick. `assets-source/refs/waterfall/approval.json` is a pending request with nothing approved. Notes and geometry: `assets-source/refs/waterfall/PLAN.md`.

- Sheet: `docs/checkpoints/waterfall/refs-sheet.jpg`. The top row shows the approved granite and river refs and the in-engine soil cliff column the face covers. Then one row per drop.
- Candidates: `assets-source/refs/waterfall/ref-1..10.jpg`. ref-1 to 3 are 4 m, ref-4 to 6 and ref-10 are 8 m, and ref-7 to 9 are 12 m.
- Your RQ2 answer is carried through: a straight lip and one sheet at 4 m, and stepped ledges in two or three tiers at 8 and 12 m. The falling sheet and the pool foam in the images are runtime water (RQ5), and the boulders and reeds round the pool are runtime scatter.

- **WF-Q1. Picks.** Recommended: ref-1 as the design (rounded pale granite with lichen and moss either side of one sheet, matching the boulders and the massif), ref-4's single ledge for 8 m, ref-7's three tiers for 12 m, and ref-10 as the eye-level and layout guide (the rock face set into the soil column, with soil beyond it on both sides). Take only the wet streaks and glossy wet stone from ref-2. ref-3 is the alternative with fewer, larger blocks. No image reaches a true 12 m against the tiles, so height comes from the geometry, not the refs.
- **WF-Q2. Natural lip or dressed coping?** ref-2, 5 and 8 put a course of squared dressed blocks on the lip, which reads as a built weir. Recommended: a natural granite lip, as in ref-1, 4, 7 and 10.
- **WF-Q3. The sheet on the ledges.** At 8 and 12 m the water breaks on each ledge, so one runtime ribbon from lip to pool would cut through the rock. Recommended: the piece exports each tier's lip as a socket, and the runtime sheet draws one ribbon per tier. This changes the water work in the plan's phase 4.
- **WF-Q4. Face width.** Recommended: 14 m as specified. That covers the whole channel cut and leaves about 1 m of soil column at each corner, as ref-9 and ref-10 show. The other option is the full 16 m side, which needs corner pieces where the face meets the column's other sides.
