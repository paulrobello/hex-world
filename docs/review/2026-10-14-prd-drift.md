## Flagged deviations

- **F1. Car cap ignores the quality tier.** §7.2 says agent caps scale with the tier. Boids do (`crates/hex-render/src/boids/plan.rs:43-55`), but every tier builds the car sim from `SimConfig::default()` (`crates/hex-app/src/sim.rs:71,329`), a fixed 300 (`crates/hex-sim/src/sim.rs:19`). The PRD now says so in §7.2 and §9.2.
- **F2. LOD cross-fade does not dither in colour.** §9.1 asks for a dithered cross-fade. The 10 m `VisibilityRange` bands at 150 m and 400 m (`crates/hex-render/src/scene.rs:67-85`) dither in the prepass (`assets/shaders/kit_prepass.wesl:217-218`), but `assets/shaders/kit_material.wesl` never calls the dither, so both LODs draw in colour across the band.
- **F3. Approval records lag the owner's answers.** The latest `approvals.json` entry still reads pending for the five road pieces and `house_pad` ("verge sink normals"; the owner approved the verge in round 14, R6), for both river mouth bases ("grass-line"; approved in round 15), and for `fish_trout` (approved with the seam fix in round 14, R5). `river_edge`, `river_straight_base` and `river_bend_base` have no piece approval record although A2 approved the family's shape, seams and LODs. The P2 "MVP tiles approved" item stays open until the records and the outstanding approvals (the spring and mouth pieces, and the river centres rebuilt in `9ddb35f`) are settled.
- **F4. CI does not run the initial-download gate.** §13 says CI runs the gates. `.github/workflows/ci.yml` runs `fmt-check`, `ui-check`, `lint`, `typecheck`, `test` and `web-size`, but not `initial-download`, which runs only in local `make checkall-ci`. `make audio-check` runs in neither `checkall` nor CI (§11.5).
- **F5. Saved default ambience volume disagrees with the web.** `SavedSettings::default` in `crates/hex-app/src/persist.rs:115-125` says it mirrors the web layer's defaults but sets ambience to 0.7, while `web/ui/src/audio.ts:95` defaults it to 0.1 (owner, 2026-10-14). The web never applies saved volumes (A16), so nothing audible changes.
- **F6. Stale comment.** `crates/hex-render/src/wind.wesl:1-2` still says tree sway comes later; `9ddb35f` made trees, wildflowers, reeds and cattails sway in this field.
