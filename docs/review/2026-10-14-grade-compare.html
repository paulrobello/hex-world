Colour grade comparison, 2026-10-14

Owner feedback: the TonyMcMapface look feels washed out next to the earlier ACES look; something between the two. Five presets, each shot live from the same native frames (1600x1000, seed 1, fixed clocks, tilt-shift off, photo mode with depth of field off). The default stays tony; nothing here changes it. Click an image for full size.

All three in-between presets bring the grass, roof and sea colour back to the ACES level and keep the river-mouth walls readable; the forest and river water stay below ACES. Grass saturation is 0.302 under tony and 0.364 under aces; rich-1, rich-2 and rich-3 read 0.373, 0.367 and 0.358. Forest canopy reads 0.458, 0.457 and 0.424 against aces 0.559 (tony 0.368), and river water 0.256, 0.253 and 0.274 against 0.290. The mouth walls read 12.1, 9.0 and 8.5/255 against 0.6/255 under aces. They differ in contrast: rich-1 keeps tony's light shadows, rich-2 deepens them part way, and rich-3 is the ACES curve with only the black floor lifted.

tony reproduces the shipped look: against the 2026-10-14 o-shipped crops the walls read 12.2 and 12.0 (12.1, 12.0), the grass 125.3 (124.9), the backdrop 16.0 (16.0) and the bridge face 31.1 (31.1). A frame taken before any set_grade matches it within 0.7/255 on every crop (default rows in measurements.txt). aces reproduces the ACES frames with today's lighter soil: walls 0.65/255, grass 128.0, backdrop 15.4.

Why these tools. Bevy grades on exposed linear values before the tone curve, except post-saturation. Section contrast pivots at 0.5 linear, so any contrast above 1 turns the walls (about 0.004) black again; no preset uses it. Bevy also blends the shadow/midtone boundary into the highlights section, so every preset keeps the shadows and highlights sections equal to avoid a seam at level 0.1.

rich-1 is tony with post-tonemap saturation 1.2. Brightness is unchanged and the walls stay at 12/255. rich-2 adds a log contrast about 0.18 linear, (c × 1.135)1.08, which deepens shade (walls 9.0, bridge face 27.3, forest 62 against 67) and keeps the grass at its tony brightness, plus post-saturation 1.12. rich-3 is ACES at EV100 12.5 with +0.013 linear added below the curve (shadows and highlights sections), which lifts the walls from 0.6 to 8.5/255 and the bridge face from 7.8 to 17.8; the rest of the frame stays within about 4/255 of aces.

Each preset carries its own backdrop clear colour, because the backdrop passes through the same curve and grading: all five read 15.3 to 16.0/255.

Frames were shot under heavy host load from other agents (1-minute load 30 to 41 after a bounded wait); every frame was checked and none was black or empty. No timings were taken.

How to switch

Settings
Settings, Graphics tab, Colour grade: pick a row; it applies at once and is remembered in this browser (not in the world save).
Web address
?grade=rich-2 (overrides the remembered choice for that visit).
Native
cargo run -p hex-app --release -- --scenario play --grade rich-2; --tonemap and --ev100 still override the preset's own values.
Live, from the console or a script
hexWorld.cmd({cmd: 'set_grade', grade: 'rich-2'}); hexWorld.state().grade reports the active preset and the list.
