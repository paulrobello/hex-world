# Raised tiles (P4, owner 2026-11-13)

The owner lifted the deferral on raised versions of the other land tiles: "placing tiles like
windmill should elevate them to matching edge heights". Forest, oak, farm, wheat field, houses
(`house_cluster`), bakery and windmill are now raisable.

`raised-shots.json` (generated by a script, then run with
`make shots-piece SCRIPT=docs/checkpoints/raised-tiles/raised-shots.json OUT=docs/checkpoints/raised-tiles ARGS='--scenario play --clock fixed --water_clock fixed'`)
builds one world. Row r = 0 holds, at level 1, a raised Meadow bridge, then forest, oak, farm,
wheat field, house cluster, bakery and a straight road. Behind it is a row of level-1 Meadows with a
hollow at (5, -1). In front, row r = 1 is level-0 Meadow, so every row-0 tile meets the ground at a
cliff. The script then drops a windmill into the hollow with no level given.
`raised-world-state.json` records it auto-placed at level 1. This hollow is fully ringed by level-1
ground. The owner's screenshot had cliffs on three sides. hex-core test
`a_windmill_in_a_three_sided_hollow_takes_level_one` covers that case: three level-1 neighbours
and open cells elsewhere give level 1. If a level-0 tile also touches the slot, every level has a
cliff, and placement takes the fewest cliff faces, then the smallest drop, then the higher level
(owner O1, 2026-11-13): the windmill stays at level 1 with one cliff.
