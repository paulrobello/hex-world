# title-theme

- **Use:** title screen and main menu. It plays once, then the game crossfades into gameplay.
- **Length:** 2:00–2:30. **BPM:** 88. **Key:** D major. **Loop:** no. Ask for a soft, resolved ending.
- **Role:** introduces the world's motif, a simple rising 5-note melody (D–E–F#–A–B), which the other cues echo.
