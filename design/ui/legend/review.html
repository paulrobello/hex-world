Hex World, Legend: the full mockup pass

Every screen and state in inventory.md, built as working prototypes in the owner-approved Legend direction. Open any screen and use it: Tab for tiles, R to turn, click to place, ⌘Z or Ctrl+Z to undo, B to bulldoze, H to hide the HUD, P for photo mode, F6 to move focus between panels. Each state below links straight to itself; press ` on any prototype for its state strip. Shots are 1440 × 900 Playwright captures (JPEG, quality 88); the clips show the motion.

What is real and what stands in

  • Play, first run and loading stand on native play-scenario captures in docs/checkpoints/ui-captures/ (scripts ui-play.json and ui-first-run.json), shot in photo mode at the play camera's angle so the engine's own slot outlines stay out of them. The play world is 14 real tiles; the first run is the scenario's single meadow. Photo, settings, worlds and the unsupported still keep the water look-dev and tilt-shift captures (docs/checkpoints/water/, docs/shots/tilt-shift/).
  • Slot outlines are the real cells projected through the same camera, and each slot's needs are its real neighbours' edges. Legality is computed live from the socket table and tile data (data/sockets.ron, data/tiles/*.ron). Rotation counts are distinct orientations, so symmetric tiles such as River straight show 1.
  • Placements the engine was captured making show the engine's pixels, clipped to the cell's projected prism: on play, River bend at the river slot, Meadow and River straight at the grass slot, Beach at the coast slot, and the removed meadow; on the first run, Meadow at any of the six slots. Any other tile or rotation falls back to a flat stand-in, and a caption on screen says so. The engine has no rise animation yet, so the rise motion over the captured tile is the mock's (in-world.md).
  • The undo history starts with three seeded placements. Undoing one updates the tile count but leaves its tile in the capture.
  • Tile glyphs are drawn from each tile's socket string until rendered thumbnails exist (F4).
  • The loading and unsupported screens reference the shipped app icon (web/brand/icon-512.png) in place, so an icon update flows through.
  • The browser in use on the unsupported page, file names, sizes and timings are illustrative values.
