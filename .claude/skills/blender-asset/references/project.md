# Hex World project specifics

Everything in this file is specific to Hex World. When the skill is promoted to user level, this file stays with the project (or becomes a per-project overlay) and `SKILL.md` keeps only the portable pipeline. The frontmatter `description` in `SKILL.md` also names Hex World and is the one line to rewrite at promotion.

## Owner rulings (R1–R3, R9)
- **R1.** `.blend` sources and bake intermediates stay local, in the gitignored blend store (below), referenced by SHA-256 in provenance. External storage is deferred. The look-dev HDRI is gitignored too: `make fetch-hdri` downloads it into `assets-source/hdri/` and verifies the SHA-256 in `assets-source/lookdev/lookdev.json`.
- **R2.** Higgsfield reference images (meadow base, road straight edge/center, hero tree) are generated and owner-approved before any modeling.
- **R3.** Authoring agents are Opus 5.5 subagents. Every provenance records `"authoringAgent": {"harness": "claude-code", "model": "claude-opus-5-5"}`. At most 3 Blender asset subagents run concurrently.
- **R9.** Every timing measurement runs under `e2e/perf-load.mjs` (load ≤ 15 before and after).
- Owner checkpoints are reviewed from screenshots.

## Frame and pivot
- Constants come from `lib.hexgeo`: R = 16 m, step = 4 m, apothem 13.856406 m.
- Pointy-top, with edge 0 facing +x. Edges run clockwise viewed from +Y, and +z is south (`crates/hex-core/src/axial.rs`).
- Pivot at the hex center. Edge pieces are authored for edge 0, which faces Blender +X.
- Blender (x, y, z) exports as runtime (x, z, -y).
- Edge k's outward normal in Blender XY is at angle −60°·k.
- Base: cap border ring at y = 0 (runtime) and skirt bottom at y = −4.0. Tree: trunk base at the origin.

## Seams
- Pieces that touch the hex border build every LOD from `hexgeo.border_ring(BORDER_SUBDIV)`.
- Never decimate a border-touching piece. Regenerate it at a lower interior density instead.
- Road cross-sections at the edge are mirror-symmetric about the lane center.
- **Water beds** follow `docs/kit/water-contract.md` (the river and coast streams): every edge's bed profile depends only on its socket, and the engine computes depth and shore inputs from those analytic profiles, never from the kit geometry. So a bed is a requirement, not a look: check every exported LOD against its profile in the generator (`kit/_coast.py` `bed_profile_errors`: border exactly on it, the ocean floor within +0.10 m above -4.0).
- **A base ring below y = 0.** A base's cap ring may sit only at a contract level (`kit_gate` `BASE_RING_LEVELS`: 0 for Grass and Forest, -`LEVEL_STEP` for Ocean), flat all round. The lib's border helpers (`cdt_cap`, `set_border_normals_up`, `lod.assert_border_identical`) work at z = 0, so build each LOD there and then move its **vertex data** down (`mesh.transform`), never a node transform: the gate reads raw accessor positions (`ocean_base.py`). A new flat level (a deeper ocean) needs `BASE_RING_LEVELS` and the skirt-bottom check in `kit_gate.rs` to change with it. A coast base's ring follows the coast profile instead and has its own gate branch (`kit_gate` `coast_of_base`; `_beach.py`, `pitfalls.md` Water beds).

## Textures
- The GLB embeds no images. Maps are PNG files named `<piece>.<channel>.png`, with channel in {albedo, normal, orm, emissive, masks}.
- albedo and emissive are sRGB. normal, orm and masks are linear (Non-Color).
- **Runtime obligation.** The PNG fallback carries no color space. PNGs for normal, orm and masks must load with `is_srgb = false`, and albedo and emissive with `is_srgb = true`. The KTX2 files carry their own vkFormat.
- masks is R snow, G wetness, B night emissive, A baked AO (owner-approved; 1.0 where a unique ORM carries the AO, such as leaf cards).
- **Walls under world-space tiling.** `kit_common::tile_frame` maps wall V to −y / tileM, so the cap rim (y = 0) sits exactly on the tile's V wrap, and tileM sets how many times the tile repeats down the 4 m skirt. A vertically profiled set measures depth below the rim exactly, t = fract(−V) with V recovered as atan2(w, z) / 2π (top PNG row = rim), so the rim row and the foot share the wrap and only a feature's lower edge may be jittered. Soil uses tileM 4.0, one profile per 4 m wall (owner, 256 px/m there), in the owner's O4 cutaway look (2026-10-13, `mat_soil.py`): a grass lip, root-dark topsoil, a warm brown body darkening softly into a near-black foot, no strata bands. The water tiles' procedural cut walls (`assets/shaders/water_terrain.wesl`, the `cap < 0.5` branch) imitate the same profile on world height, rim at y = 0 and foot 4 m down, so retune both together.
- Each map ships as `<name>.<channel>.{bc7.ktx2,astc.ktx2,png}`. UASTC q4 + RDO + zstd 18 source, transcoded to BC7 (vkFormat 145 linear / 146 sRGB) and ASTC 4x4 (157 / 158), with a full mip chain.
- Texel density (D2): bases and road surfaces use a world-space tileable set at 512 px/m plus a unique `masks` bake at 32 px/m. Props and tree leaves use a unique atlas at ≥ 256 px/m median (measured). Density is checked **per material**, never as one pooled median, so many small faces of one material cannot hide another's low density (`kit_gate::texel_density_is_checked_per_material`).
- **Solid-dome canopy (tree, owner ruling; card-only canopies failed the look).** Clumps are closed low-poly dome shells (slot `{"unique": true}`, no masks) carrying a bake of one high-poly master dome covered in real leaf geometry over a solid dark dome at 0.97 R (`_tree.canopy_high`, saved in the `.blend` as `<id>_canopy_high`); alpha cards are only a thin fringe on the clump rims and undersides, each facing straight out from its clump centre. To keep the shell bake at ≥ 256 px/m in a 2048² atlas, the master and the shell are SECTORS-fold symmetric (3) and every sector shares one UV island set, and every clump is a transformed copy of the one baked shell. Set the shell's custom normals to the smooth dome's own normals before the bake (`shell_smooth_normals`), so the map carries leaf relief rather than low-poly facets; bend them toward up and out of the canopy after the bake. Since the P2 polish the shell slot samples masks too (snow, wetness and night hooks reach the domes), so its islands pack around the locked bark islands with a gutter (`pack_below_strip(locked=…)` in `tree_hero_oak.py`), and the masks carry A = 1 on the canopy texels (their AO stays in ORM.R) and R snow capacity from the canopy AO. Two shells-level rules from that pass (`kit/_oak.py`): keep low-frequency relief OUT of the baked master (a SECTORS-fold lump makes the 120° repeat readable on one dome) and put seeded, non-symmetric lumps into each clump's shell geometry instead, with the lumped surface's analytic normals per clump (`shell_normals`) so every LOD shades as a smooth dome; build LOD1/LOD2 shells by decimating each CLOSED clump shell, never the culled LOD0 canopy, whose open rims decimate into spikes, and do not cull a coarse LOD's faces against a neighbour's smooth surface (its decimated polyhedron sits well inside it, so the cull opens gaps). The dark dome showing between the baked leaves takes roughness 1.0 and a darker albedo after the bake (`GAP_*` in `tree_hero_oak.py`), and `kit_material.wesl` fades F0 to 0 on unique foliage above roughness 0.95, so the gaps carry no sky sheen; every other foliage roughness stays below 0.95.
- **Foliage normals.** A slot with `alphaCutoff` (leaf cards) shades its back faces with the front normal (`kit_material.wesl`, `kit_prepass.wesl`), instead of Bevy's double-sided flip. Author card normals as one rounded canopy: blend the directions from the clump centre and the canopy centre with an up bias, and keep half the card's face normal so MikkTSpace never gets a normal in the card plane (`_tree.set_canopy_normals`).
- **Tree bark is a tileable set on UV0** (owner ruling, hero oak look pass): the `bark` set (1024² over 2 m = 512 px/m, `scripts/blender/kit/mat_bark.py`, `make kit-material SET=bark`) bound through the slot `{"tileable": "bark", "tileUvScale": K, "masks": true}`. World-space tiling cannot map a vertical cylinder (its tangent frame degenerates), so the bark is unwrapped per branch in metres (U around, V along: the grain follows every strand), scaled by `uvPerM` and packed unrotated into the piece atlas. At runtime StandardMaterial's `uv_transform` multiplies UV0 by K = 1 / (uvPerM · tileM) for the set's albedo, normal and ORM (forward and prepass), while `masks` keeps raw UV0, so the bark's own AO lives in masks.A. No binding is added: the set occupies StandardMaterial's existing three slots, and forward/prepass stay at 16/7 (web-smoke runtime-kit case). The oak uses uvPerM = 1/54 on its 1x build and ships at 1.2x, so K = 1.2 / (uvPerM · tileM) = 32.4 (size pass, 2026-10-14; 1/36 and K = 18 in P2, 1/24 and K = 12 before): bark 512 px/m median, and AO 32 px/m in a 2048² masks. The bark UV is metric on the low-poly surface (`_oak.metric_tube_uv`: chord U, one V per ring). The bark islands pack into a left column of the atlas so the canopy islands get one near-square block beside them (a bottom band left the square shell island set height-limited).

## Budgets
Kit LOD0 ≤ 4000 tris and tree ≤ 3000. LOD1 ≤ 50% and LOD2 ≤ 15% of LOD0. GLB ≤ 2 MiB (2,097,152 B). Maps ≤ 2048². Full table: `gates.md`.

Buildings (houses) are kind `prop`, because PRD §6 lists houses among the props: LOD0 ≤ 1,500 tris, KTX2 class `piece`. A unique 256 px/m atlas for a house (about 150 m²) would need about 4096², far over that budget. So a house samples the world-space `plaster` set tinted per slot for its isotropic surfaces, models directional detail (tile courses, timber framing, window frames) as geometry, and bakes only a unique 512² `masks` (AO, plus night B on the glass). See `scripts/blender/kit/_house.py`.

**Rotors (animated props, PRD §7.2).** A part that turns as a rigid transform (the windmill's sails) is a node and mesh `<id>_rotor_lod<n>` beside each `<id>_lod<n>` (`hex_assets::rotor_mesh_name`), in every LOD. Put the node on the axle with its local +X along it (a tilt goes in the node's rotation) and author the mesh round local X: the runtime draws it at placement × node and turns it about local X (`kit_scene::spin_rotors`, `ROTOR_RPM`, phase from the hub's position, on the app clock), and the gate fails a rotor whose vertex centroid sits more than 1 cm off that axis. Each LOD's budget is body plus rotor (`kit_gate::rotor_tris_count_toward_the_lod_budget`). `export.write_export_report` counts only `<id>_lod<n>`, so the generator folds the rotor into `lodTris` (provenance takes one entry per LOD from the key's last digit). Bake the body and the rotor as separate objects so no shadow turns with the part, and build the collider from the still body only. The kit scenario spins rotors; `play.rs` loads only `<id>_lod<n>`, so a rotor piece placed in play needs that path extended first (`kit/windmill.py`).

**Props** (manifest `kind: "prop"`, LOD0 ≤ 1,500 tris; the river dressing props 2,500, `gates.md`) can only use the `piece` class. An opaque baked prop ships four 512² maps (`rock_small`: 0.56 MB per family); an alpha-card prop ships a 1024² albedo plus a 512² normal (below). At 512² the 256 px/m density floor leaves room for only about 2 m² of textured surface, buried faces included: cut faces that sit below the bury depth before unwrapping (`rock_small` keeps 0.3 m below the pivot, enough for the meadow relief's −0.25 m dips). A larger or multi-stone rock takes a 1024² albedo with normal and ORM box-filtered to 512² (`rock_small_b`, `rock_small_c`: about 1.1-1.2 MB per family). Maps may differ in size. An alpha-card prop keeps a 1024² albedo, which carries the alpha edges and sets the measured density, and ships its normal at 512² (`wildflower_clump`: a 1024² normal alone took 0.59 MB and pushed the piece to 99% of its budget).

**Variants (PRD §6 Variety).** A variant is its own piece and GLB with its own maps, named `<original>_b`, `_c`, …, in the original's pack and with its kind, slot flags and `wind` amplitude. The manifest has no variant field: the runtime groups them in `crates/hex-render/src/play/scatter.rs` `VARIANTS` (a tile rule names the original; each instance draws one piece of the group from a hash of the tile seed), and every variant needs its own `FOOTPRINTS` entry and must be added wherever the original is listed (walk `OBSTACLES`, grass `GROWS_THROUGH`, boids `canopy_perches`). The original's generator and `_*.py` helpers are frozen by its approval, so a variant never edits them and cannot import a monolithic generator: put the variant pipeline in a new family helper that reuses the frozen helpers' functions (`_spruce.py` sets `_forest`'s module globals before calling it, `_flowers.py` draws with `_props`' painters) and copy only the monolithic steps (`_rock.py`). Spruce variants draw their frond strip with the original's seed, so the needle tiles match it; a taller trunk needs a smaller bark `uvPerM` to fit below `NEEDLE_V0`, and its manifest `tileUvScale` follows (`tree_forest_b`: 1/80, 40). The single-stem wildflowers (`wildflower_single`, 14 tris) are their own family with a `vary` scatter rule.

KTX2 bytes are budgeted per family (BC7 and ASTC each) by budget class, per the owner ruling of 2026-10-13 (`KTX_BUDGETS` in `scripts/assets/kit-lib.mjs`):
- `piece` (the default): 1,500,000 B, for a piece's unique maps (final, `docs/decisions/0002-asset-pipeline.md` D3).
- `tileable`: 3,600,000 B per set. `make kit-material` assigns it automatically.
- `hero`: 11,500,000 B, for full-detail hero pieces such as the tree.

To mark a hero piece, call `export.write_export_report(..., budget_class="hero")` in its generator, which writes `"budgetClass": "hero"` into the stage's `export-report.json`. That is the durable way. A one-off run can pass `make kit-compress PIECE=<id> CLASS=hero` (`--class` on the CLI) instead. The class is recorded in `compress-report.json` under `budgets.class`, and `make kit-check` budgets the piece by that class. Hero pieces and their tileable sets ship in streamed biome packs. The 30 MB initial download keeps only the WASM, the core kit and the UI.

### The blend store (owner ruling O2, 2026-10-13)
- Every `.blend` (and any `.blend1`) lives ONLY in the **main checkout's** gitignored `assets-source/blend/<id>/`, shared by every worktree, so a worktree removal never deletes a source. The high-poly is `<store>/<id>/<id>_high.blend`. Never save a `.blend` anywhere else, including the worktree's own `assets-source/blend/`.
- Resolve it through `scripts/blender/lib/paths.py` (stdlib only, tested by `test_paths`): `blend_store()` is `$HEXW_BLEND_STORE` or the parent of `git rev-parse --path-format=absolute --git-common-dir`, plus `assets-source/blend`; `blend_path(id)` and `blend_key(path)` (store-relative). CLI: `python3 scripts/blender/lib/paths.py store | blend <id> | key <path>`.
- Users: the export stage's `BLEND` default, the live save step, and `make kit-provenance ROUTE=interactive` (which defaults `BLEND` and passes `--blend-store`). Provenance records `blendRef {store: "local:assets-source/blend", key: "<id>/<id>_high.blend", sha256, bytes}`, never a host path.
- `.gitignore` ignores `*.blend` and `*.blend[0-9]*` everywhere as a backstop.
- If a `.blend` already sits in a worktree, `mv` it into the store (no second copy) and confirm its SHA-256 is unchanged.

## Tree wind data (TEXCOORD_1, controller ruling 2026-10-13)
Trees carry per-vertex wind data for a later vertex-shader wind pass (PRD §9.1, P2), so the asset needs no re-authoring when wind lands.
- **Attribute.** Blender UV map 2, named `wind`, exported as glTF `TEXCOORD_1` (FLOAT) on every primitive of every `<id>_lod{0,1,2}`. `UVMap` (TEXCOORD_0, the atlas) stays first and active: create `wind` after the atlas unwrap and pack.
- **U = sway weight** in [0, 1]: 0 at the trunk base (and on roots), rising along the branch hierarchy (trunk top 0.12, limb tips 0.65, twig tips 0.88, tertiary tips 1.0, each child starting at its parent's value at the fork), and toward 1 on the clump's outer leaf cards. Canopy dome shells carry `SHELL_SWAY` (0.35) times their clump's sway, one value per clump (the solid domes move less than their fringe). Derived from the skeleton (`_tree.skeleton()` `sway`), not world height. After decimation, U below 0.01 snaps to 0 so collapsed root tips stay still.
- **V = phase** in [0, 1]: a seeded hash per leaf card (all four corners equal, so the card moves as a unit) and per branch. The glTF exporter writes V as 1 − v; phase is cyclic, so the shader uses V as read.
- **Never COLOR_0.** StandardMaterial multiplies vertex colour into base colour and would tint the tree.
- **No new bindings.** It is a vertex attribute only: no texture, and the 16/7 sampled-texture counts are unchanged.
- **Shader use (landed 2026-10-14).** `crates/hex-render/src/foliage_wind.wesl`, called from the kit vertex stages (`assets/shaders/kit_vertex.wesl` forward, `kit_prepass.wesl` for depth, normals, motion and every shadow cascade) on slots with a manifest `wind` amplitude (m per unit wind push) whose mesh carries TEXCOORD_1. The push is `hex_render::wind::sample`, the grass's field, on `globals.time` (the fixed clock steps it by 1/60 s). A tree leans from its base by (height / 10 m)² with the field sampled at the trunk base, flexes by U², and its cards with U above 0.6 flutter on V; a plant bends by U². Every slot of one piece takes the same amplitude (`kit_gate::wind_slots_carry_their_attribute`). LOD decimation interpolates U, so it is clamped back to [0, 1] after the LOD joins.
- **Plant wind data (river dressing, wildflower).** The same `wind` UV map on every LOD. U is 0 at each plant's own base (never height above the piece origin: a merged centre holds reeds at many heights beside still stones and banks) and rises to a · sqrt(H / 2 m) at its tip, where a is the fraction along the plant and H its length, so the shader's U² bend moves each tip in proportion to its height as the grass does. V is a phase per blade or stalk. A cattail's head and spike take U from their distance along the stalk axis, so they ride the stalk, and the spike's `river_stone` slot takes `wind` too (its stones carry U = 0). `_river_dress.wind_uv` writes it from bmesh vertex layers after the planar UV0 and any join. `wildflower_clump` uses the tree convention (U up to about 1 at the tips), with a smaller amplitude.
- **Trees need alpha-cutoff leaf cards.** The gate measures the sway of the fringe through the slots with `alphaCutoff`: their median U must be ≥ 0.5, and a tree with no alpha-cutoff slot fails. U must also stay ≤ 0.1 below runtime y 0.5 m.
- **Pinned by** `kit_gate::tree_wind_uv_contract` and the tree branch of `kit_gate::geometry_budgets_lods_sockets_border` (present on every tree LOD primitive, both channels in [0, 1], U = 0 on the lowest vertices). Meshopt QUANTIZE keeps it FLOAT. Debug renders: `scripts/blender/tree_wind_debug.py`.

## Tools and pins
- Blender 5.2.x at `BLENDER ?= /Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender`. The make variable `BLENDER_RUN` expands to `"$(BLENDER)" -b --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1`.
- KTX-Software 4.4.2 from `make fetch-tools` (`. tools/ktx-env.sh` puts `ktx` on PATH).
- One gltf-transform pin, in `scripts/assets/package.json`: `@gltf-transform/{core,extensions,functions,cli}@4.3.0`, plus `gltf-validator` 2.0.0-dev.3.10, `meshoptimizer` 0.25.0 and `sharp` 0.34.4. Install with `cd scripts/assets && npm ci --no-audit --no-fund`. Run the CLI from that directory with `npx --no-install gltf-transform …`, never a global install.
- **Node dependency resolution.** Only `scripts/assets/` has a `node_modules`. ESM ignores `NODE_PATH`, so a script outside `scripts/assets/` (for example `scripts/refs/*.mjs`) loads a pinned dependency with:

  ```js
  import { createRequire } from "node:module";
  const require = createRequire(new URL("../assets/package.json", import.meta.url));
  const sharp = require("sharp");
  ```
